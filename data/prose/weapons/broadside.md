## How it works

Broadside fires **to both sides of Tibo's facing**, not toward the nearest pest. Each unit of Amount makes a pair of shells at opposite right angles. The row origins are spaced 22 units along the facing line and centred around Tibo. Level 1 has raw Amount 2 plus Tibo's +1: three row origins, six shells, released at the raw 0.06-second interval. It can cast into an empty arena. <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` Cb.side=-1,wb(e,t,n,r),Cb.side=1,wb(e,t,n,r) ``]</small> <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` Cb.along=(i-(r.amount-1)/2)*n.rowGap ``]</small>

The first 32 units of flight are unarmed. At speed 170, that is about 0.188 s; the whole 56-unit flight lasts about 0.329 s. Once armed, a base shell becomes an ordinary explosive projectile for its remaining flight. A direct collision or the end of its life can trigger its blast. Shell collision radius is `3 × √Area`, but **blast radius is `18 × Area`**, so they do not grow the same way. Full Broadside's extra rows and bomblets are not part of this weapon. <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` i.armAt[a]=Math.min(n.minFlight,Cb.reach)/u,i.flight[a]=Cb.reach/u ``]</small> <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` i.radius[a]=n.size*Math.sqrt(r.area),i.explodeRadius[a]=n.explodeRadius*r.area ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-DwGb2BE_.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-mechanics-groups.mjs` used original cast fixtures to check paired perpendicular velocity, row origin, arming and flight times, and Area 1 versus 4. A full Game empty-arena control produced six shells. Damage effectiveness against a natural crowd was not measured.

## Overview

Hash Hawk keeps birds circling Tibo until they can dive at a target. Its default choice favors current HP, with extra weight for elites and bosses. It does not simply pick the nearest pest.

## How it works

- Each bird searches within 150 units of **Tibo**, including a target whose body overlaps that range. Without an active targeting override, its score is current HP × 100 for bosses, × 10 for elites, or × 1 for other pests. A sufficiently healthy ordinary pest can therefore beat a weakened elite or boss. Hidden and downed targets are excluded. Active aiming can take precedence over this default scoring; these checks do not cover every targeting rune. <small>[sim-BluGyCqn.js: ``if(cd.aiming){let i=cd.aim(e,t,n,r.range);if(i>=0)return i}``]</small> <small>[sim-BluGyCqn.js: ``u=(c?e.bosses.hp[a]:e.enemies.hp[a])*vS(r,c,l);u>s&&(s=u,o=n)``]</small> <small>[sim-BluGyCqn.js: ``return t?e.bossWeight:n?e.eliteWeight:1``]</small> <small>[start-rows-BZWEJ9T3.js: ``retrySec:.2,staggerSec:.6,eliteWeight:10,bossWeight:100``]</small>
- Amount controls the number of birds, rather than the number of simultaneous shots from one bird. Level 1 has raw Amount 1 plus Tibo's base +1, giving two birds. New birds wait 0.6 s × (their order + 1) before their first search. An empty search retries after 0.2 s. The hawk pool holds at most 16 birds. <small>[sim-BluGyCqn.js: ``i.t[a]=x(r.staggerSec*(n+1))``]</small> <small>[sim-BluGyCqn.js: ``dS=16,fS=class{capacity=dS;pool=new fa(dS)``]</small> <small>[sim-BluGyCqn.js: ``if(!V(d))return void(a.t[t]=x(n.retrySec));``]</small>
- Birds circle on a flattened orbit of radius 36, then dive at raw speed 320. A dive follows its locked target while that target remains alive with the same pool identity. If it dies or its slot is reused, the bird continues to the last destination rather than switching to the replacement. <small>[start-rows-BZWEJ9T3.js: ``range:150,circleRadius:36,circleSquash:.55,circleTurn:2.4,returnSpeed:240,burstRadius:36,twentyMinuteDailyBurstRadius:12``]</small> <small>[sim-BluGyCqn.js: ``V(s)&&gd(e,s)&&hd(e,s,o.targetId[t])?(o.tx[t]=q(e,s),o.ty[t]=J(e,s)):o.target[t]=-1``]</small>
- On arrival, the bird damages targets in a burst. The impact search radius is `36 × sqrt(effective Area) + half the locked target's radius`; Dailies before **23 November 2026 UTC** (day 20780) keep the old coefficient **12**. At Area 1 against a locked target of radius 8, that is 40 instead of 16—not triple the total radius. Other target bodies also count in the overlap check; the change is not a promise of triple damage or DPS. Only then does that bird start its weapon cooldown, so travel time is additional to the raw 2.2 s cooldown. Base Hash Hawk leaves no burning crumbs; those belong to [Hash Harrier](/weapons/hash-harrier). <small>[sim-BluGyCqn.js: ``a.hitR[t]=function(e,t){return lS(e)?t.twentyMinuteDailyBurstRadius:t.burstRadius}(e,n)*Math.sqrt(r.area)+.5*Cd(e,d)``]</small> <small>[sim-BluGyCqn.js: ``a.phase[t]=uS.circle,a.t[t]=y(i.cooldown)``]</small> <small>[sim-BluGyCqn.js: ``r.crumbs!==void 0&&function(e,t,n,r,i,a)``]</small>

## Controlled checks

Build 219 (`main-czdspc5P.js`) and its predecessor were compared in 120 supplied-state strike cases, seed `hawk-radius`, Normal difficulty: Hash Hawk and Hash Harrier, effective Area 0.5625/1.5625/2.25, locked-target radii 8/30, ordinary Standard/Story and Standard Daily days 20779/20780/20781. Original search and impact functions select the larger radius outside earlier Dailies, include an 8-radius neighbor at body-overlap equality, exclude one 0.02 units farther out and hit an additional asymmetric target only with the larger strike. Weapons, target HP/positions, Area and the bird's impact position were supplied; this checks strike coverage and compatibility, not natural acquisition, damage resolution or DPS. The historical targeting checks below were not rerun or promoted to current-build coverage.

Controlled original-engine checks passed on build 206 (`main-CrJIatJG.js`), Standard/Normal, seeds `s1`, `s2`, `s3`, using `node tools/probe-targeting-lures.mjs main-CrJIatJG.js`. Six Hash Hawk scenario groups covered weighted current HP, elite/boss preference, hidden-boss exclusion, the inclusive body-overlap range boundary, moving locks and reused-slot rejection. Full Game runs observed two birds, first dives at ticks 36 and 72, impact followed by a 132-tick cooldown, and no burning crumbs. A supplied raw Amount of 30 saturated the 16-bird pool.

The script grants level-1 Hash Hawk, inserts fixed-HP aphids and a boss-pool fixture, and uses an export-only bridge to the original client for isolated targeting cases. It also steps the original Game for attack timing. It uses no gear, clock warp or HP refill. This is not natural acquisition, representative DPS, a test of every targeting rune, or proof of server behavior. The [coverage inventory](/coverage#mechanics-inventory) binds the retained scenario to this source, definition and script revision.

## Overview

Spud Decoy plants timed lures behind Tibo's facing direction. They attract nearby pests and explode when they expire. They do not burst early just because a pest reaches them.

## How it works

- Each cast plants decoys 26 units from Tibo, centered behind his facing direction. Extra Amount fans them out with an angular spacing of 1.1 radians. Level 1 has raw Amount 1 plus Tibo's +1, giving two decoys per cast. Raw cooldown is 7 s. <small>[start-rows-CGx2Fz7i.js: ``I={lure:{reach:80},behind:26,spread:1.1,burstRadius:30}``]</small> <small>[sim--p06GhX-.js: ``s=w(o.fy[e.me],o.fx[e.me])+Math.PI+(i-(r.amount-1)/2)*n.spread``]</small> <small>[sim--p06GhX-.js: ``Jd(e,o.x[e.me]+C(s)*n.behind,o.y[e.me]+E(s)*n.behind,qd(n.lure,r.area),a(r.duration),t.slot)``]</small>
- Lure reach is `80 × sqrt(effective Area)`, measured between centers. Only enemies strictly inside qualify; the nearest active lure wins. Lure steering stops them within 7 units. This is the same shared lure system as [Peel Phantom](/weapons/peel-phantom), including its limits: enemy behavior runs afterward and can override movement, and this enemy-loop redirect is not a boss taunt. <small>[sim--p06GhX-.js: ``function qd(e,t){return e.reach*Math.sqrt(t)}``]</small> <small>[sim--p06GhX-.js: ``u>=d*d||u>=a||(i=s,a=u)``]</small> <small>[sim--p06GhX-.js: ``a&&dx(e,s),Qd(e,s),n.timer[s]=WE(n.timer[s],t),KO(e,s,v.enemyAt(n.def[s]).behavior,u)``]</small>
- Raw duration is 4 s, or 5 s with Tibo's base ×1.25 Duration. At the end tick the decoy stops attracting and explodes for weapon damage in radius `30 × sqrt(effective Area)`. Its owner's weapon processes the explosion, then removes the lure. Base Spud Decoy does not leave the second, smaller decoy produced by [Clockwork Spud](/weapons/clockwork-spud). <small>[sim--p06GhX-.js: ``if(i.owner[o]!==t.slot||!Xd(e,o))continue;``]</small> <small>[sim--p06GhX-.js: ``ed(e,i.x[o],i.y[o],n.burstRadius*Math.sqrt(r.area)*c),Nb(e,t,n,r,o),Zd(e,o)``]</small> <small>[sim--p06GhX-.js: ``let o=n.echo,s=B(e).lures;if(o===void 0||s.echo[i]===1)return;``]</small>

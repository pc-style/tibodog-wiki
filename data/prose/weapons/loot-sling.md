## Overview

Loot Sling unites [Gem Catapult](/weapons/gem-catapult) and [Bottomless Sack](/weapons/bottomless-sack). Own both evolutions and open a [chest](/chests) to combine them and free one weapon slot. It pulls random evolved attacks automatically, then spends collected XP gems on extra attacks aimed along Tibo's heading.

## How it works

- **Fire without waiting for gems.** When a pest is nearby, each automatic volley makes two random evolved picks per Amount. With raw Amount 1 and Tibo's base +1, that is four picks every 1.6 s before cooldown bonuses. Without a target within 70% of the larger field dimension, it retries after 0.1 s rather than spending a volley. <small>[sim-CVTo8bBU.js: `let a=.7*Math.max(e.field.width,e.field.height)`]</small> <small>[sim-CVTo8bBU.js: `mk.aim=NaN,mk.n=a,mk.count=n.count,e.sling.shots+=hk(e,t,n,r,i)`]</small>
- **Collect gems for extra picks.** XP pickups enter a queue capped at 30. It spends one queued gem at a time, at a raw interval of 0.6 s before interval bonuses; each gem makes one evolved pick per Amount, two with no Amount bonuses. Gold does not count. This is separate from the automatic volley, and does not need a nearby target. Releasing and reacquiring the sling clears old pickup debt. <small>[sim-CVTo8bBU.js: `a.queue=Math.min(n.maxQueue,a.queue+e.counters.gems-a.seenGems)`]</small> <small>[sim-CVTo8bBU.js: `if(s.queue<=0||e.tick-s.pulledAt<o(i.interval))return`]</small>
- **Use the sling's stats, not the copied weapon's damage.** Copies use Loot Sling's raw damage 23, Area 1.2, speed 230, Duration 1.2 s, pierce 2 and knockback 1, with Tibo's bonuses. The chosen evolved weapon supplies the attack shape and its special parameters. Amount is set to 1 inside each copy: extra Amount adds picks, not another multiplier within every picked shape. <small>[sim-CVTo8bBU.js: `pk.damage=e.damage,pk.cooldown=e.cooldown,pk.amount=1,pk.area=e.area`]</small>
- **Face the pests for pickup shots.** Gem-triggered picks set an aim override along Tibo's heading, fanned 0.16 radians apart with up to 0.05 radians of random spread. They exclude beam shapes. Target-seeking shapes use this override; shapes with their own pattern, such as upward lobs, retain that pattern. Automatic picks use the ordinary evolved pool and normal targeting. <small>[sim-CVTo8bBU.js: `` n.weapons.filter(e=>e.behavior.kind!==`beam`) ``]</small> <small>[sim-CVTo8bBU.js: `if(!Number.isNaN(e.attack.aim))return e.attack.aim`]</small>

Direct original-function controls check Amount 1/2/5, two automatic picks versus one pickup pick per Amount, copied Amount 1, raw damage 23, queue overflow, 35/36-tick pickup boundaries, heading spread, non-beam pickup shapes, no-target retries and aim reset. ## Tips

- **Keep collecting XP while fighting.** Automatic volleys work without pickups, but each queued gem supplies more evolved attacks.
- **Stack Amount for both firing modes.** Every extra Amount adds two automatic picks per volley and one pick per spent gem.

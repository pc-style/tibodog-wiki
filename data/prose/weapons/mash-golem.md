## How it works

Mash Golem waits for a target within its 120-unit search range before beginning a cast, but **walks along Tibo's facing**, rather than homing after that target. Each golem starts 10 units ahead. Extra golems fan alternately to either side in 24° steps. Level 1 has two golems after Tibo's +1 Amount; they appear at the 0.3-second interval, not simultaneously. The shared pool holds at most eight. <small>[sim-Dk46XOqX.js: `` return xd(e,e.player.x[e.me],e.player.y[e.me],t.reach)!==void 0 ``]</small> <small>[sim-Dk46XOqX.js: `` return e===0?0:(e%2==1?1:-1)*Math.ceil(e/2)*t*zS ``]</small>

A golem first rises for 0.3 s, then walks at raw speed 50 for its effective Duration (1.5 s at level 1 with Tibo's base bonus). Its body radius is `11 × √Area`. While walking, body pulses every 0.15 s damage overlapping pests and shove them to the side of its path. After walking ends, it slams once for **2.5 times its damage**, with radius `28 × √Area` and 60% of its ordinary knockback, then disappears. Mash Colossus's slow patches and expanding ring are not base-golem effects. <small>[sim-Dk46XOqX.js: `` if(r.age[o]>i+r.walkTicks[o]){US(e,r,o,t,n.area);continue} ``]</small> <small>[sim-Dk46XOqX.js: `` Y(e,t.damage[n]*r.slamMult,t.src[n],t.slot[n],.6*t.knock[n]) ``]</small>

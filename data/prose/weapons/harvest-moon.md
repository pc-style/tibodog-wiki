## How it works

Harvest Moon keeps blades orbiting at `56 × √Area`, with collision radius `6 × Area`. It is continuously active, rather than alternating a cooldown and active period. Overlapping targets share a per-target 0.5-second strike gate across its blades. Projectile-speed bonuses increase angular speed at half their usual excess rate. <small>[sim-DpazMjS2.js: `` return e.orbitRadius*Math.sqrt(t.area) ``]</small> <small>[sim-DpazMjS2.js: `` let a=e.player.x[e.me],o=e.player.y[e.me],s=Math.round(60*n.strikeInterval) ``]</small>

The blades can also **pick up ordinary pests with mass below 1** within `7 × Area` of a blade. Bosses and mass-1 pests are excluded. A carried pest follows the rotating blade, with walking velocity and existing knockback cleared. Carry time is at least 0.4 s or the weapon's effective Duration, whichever is longer; level 1 with Tibo's base Duration gives 1.125 s, rounded to 68 ticks. After release it cannot be grabbed again for three more seconds. Pool-generation checks prevent a replacement pest in a reused slot from inheriting that carry. <small>[sim-DpazMjS2.js: `` if(a.mass[f]>=1)continue; ``]</small> <small>[sim-DpazMjS2.js: `` o.until[f]=e.tick+l,o.freeAt[f]=e.tick+l+u ``]</small>

Release queues another weapon hit and flings the pest outward with speed `240 × knockback multiplier × (1 − mass)`. Hunter's Moon's later burst is not present here. <small>[sim-DpazMjS2.js: `` u=n.fling*e.loadout.stats.knockbackMult*(1-a.mass[i]) ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-DwGb2BE_.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-mechanics-groups.mjs` supplied mass 0.2 versus mass 1 targets, checked 68-tick carry, the three-second lock, rotating placement and release damage/192-speed fling. These original-function fixtures are not a natural survival or boss-control demonstration.

## Overview

Skillet Flail swings a skillet through pests in front of and behind Tibo. It starts at 14 raw damage and a 0.76 s cooldown. At level 8, hold [Starch Plating](/passives/starch-plating) at any level and open a [chest](/chests) to evolve it into [Iron Flail](/weapons/iron-flail).

## How it works

The `twin` slash consumer emits a pair of opposite swings for each volley entry. Its raw swing dimensions are 52 × 22, scaled by modified Area. Raw Pierce 99 maps to the game's unlimited-pierce sentinel, not a literal 99-pest promise. More Amount adds paired swings through the volley system; it does not turn the attack into a single forward-only slash. <small>[sim-BsNpoIrY.js: `!0===n.twin?(Jk(e,t,n,r,2*i),Jk(e,t,n,r,2*i+1)):Jk(e,t,n,r,i)`]</small>

Skillet Flail is melee-tagged but explicitly excluded from melee-restricted new-weapon offers and melee starter fill. It is eligible in ordinary offers, including Mech Pilot's unrestricted pool. See [Modes](/modes#new-melee-weapons-and-set-restrictions).

**Controlled checks:** original `main-CAVio-MR.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `wiki`, `s1`, `s2`, granted level-1 weapon, one fixed high-HP pest on each horizontal side and an out-of-reach control: one emitted volley entry hit both side targets and not the distant pest. Offer/start and chest fixtures verify eligibility and the recipe; they do not establish natural acquisition odds, practical DPS or survival.

## Tips

- **Use both sides of the swing** when pests approach from opposite directions.
- **Starch Plating is the catalyst**, not Masher Grip or Salt Shaker.

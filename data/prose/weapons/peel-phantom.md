## Overview

Peel Phantom leaves a husk at the start of a dash. Nearby pests can chase it while it lasts; it explodes when its time runs out. Standing still does not plant husks, despite the zero cooldown in its stat table.

## How it works

- A successful dash plants `max(1, round(effective Amount))` husks at its starting point. Extra husks spread sideways across the dash direction, 18 units apart. The base weapon has raw Amount 0, so Tibo's +1 still gives one husk. <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``return e.player.dashedAt[e.me]===e.tick``]</small> <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``l=Math.max(1,Math.round(r.amount)),u=qd(n.lure,r.area),d=a(r.duration)``]</small> <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``let a=(r-(l-1)/2)*n.spread,p=Jd(e,i.dashFromX[o]-c*a,i.dashFromY[o]+s*a,u,d,t.slot)``]</small>
- Lure reach is `64 × sqrt(effective Area)`. An enemy must be **strictly inside** that distance from the husk's center; touching the boundary does not count. With several eligible lures, the nearest one wins. Within 7 units the lure steering stops the enemy. <small>[start-rows-gvsryVHP.js: ``behavior:{kind:`peelPhantom`,lure:{reach:64},burst:24,spread:18}``]</small> <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``function qd(e,t){return e.reach*Math.sqrt(t)}``]</small> <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``u>=d*d||u>=a||(i=s,a=u)``]</small> <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``if(s<7)return r.vx[t]=0,void(r.vy[t]=0)``]</small>
- This redirects enemy movement, not a universal taunt. The enemy steering loop applies lure steering before the enemy's own behavior, which can change its movement again. It is not applied to bosses by that loop. The controlled attraction checks use ordinary aphids, not every enemy family. <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``a&&dx(e,s),Qd(e,s),n.timer[s]=WE(n.timer[s],t),KO(e,s,v.enemyAt(n.def[s]).behavior,u)``]</small>
- Raw duration is 1.5 s. With Tibo's base duration multiplier of 1.25, that rounds to 113 ticks at 60 Hz. The husk does not damage pests merely for reaching it. At its end tick it stops attracting and bursts for weapon damage in radius `24 × sqrt(effective Area)`, with normal body-overlap hit checks. Base Phantom has neither the spinning cuts nor the ten thrown peels of [Peel Poltergeist](/weapons/peel-poltergeist). <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``return n.pool.isAlive(t)&&e.tick<n.endsAt[t]``]</small> <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``return n.pool.isAlive(t)&&e.tick>=n.endsAt[t]``]</small> <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``i.owner[s]===t.slot&&Xd(e,s)&&(qy(e,t,r,s,a),Xy(e,t,n,r,s),Zd(e,s))``]</small> <small>[sim-DvRnAb2l.js: ``let a=n.ring;if(a===void 0)return;``]</small>

Six Phantom scenario groups covered idle versus real-dash planting, placement at the dash start, one base husk, 113-tick life, no early contact damage and an explosion on the exact expiry tick without evolution projectiles. Isolated original-client steering fixtures checked sqrt(Area) reach, strict range equality, nearest-lure selection, the inner stop and expired-lure exclusion.

The script grants level-1 Phantom and inserts fixed-HP aphids. Cast and expiry checks step the original Game; steering fixtures call original functions through an export-only, source-hashed bridge. It uses no gear, clock warp or HP refill. This does not establish natural acquisition, survival value, every enemy AI interaction, or server behavior.

## Overview

Iron Flail evolves [Skillet Flail](/weapons/skillet-flail) with [Starch Plating](/passives/starch-plating). Open a [chest](/chests) with Skillet Flail at level 8 and the passive owned at any level.

The evolved definition has 22 raw damage, a 1.2 s cooldown, Area 1.2 and knockback 2. Its cooldown is slower than the base flail's starting 0.76 s; the wider, heavier swings do not imply a faster attack.

## How it works

Each volley entry emits opposite swings, using the same twin-slash consumer as Skillet Flail. Raw swing dimensions 60 × 26 scale with modified Area; the weapon's own Area 1.2 applies before other Area bonuses. Raw Pierce 99 maps to unlimited pierce. Extra Amount adds paired swings rather than extending one swing's reach. <small>[sim-C4Oo36Ru.js: `!0===n.twin?(dk(e,t,n,r,2*i),dk(e,t,n,r,2*i+1)):dk(e,t,n,r,i)`]</small> <small>[sim-C4Oo36Ru.js: `uk.width=n.size*r.area*c,uk.height=n.height*r.area*c`]</small>

**Controlled checks:** original `main-CAVio-MR.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `wiki`, `s1`, `s2`, granted weapon and fixed high-HP pests on either side: one emitted volley entry hit both side targets, not the distant control. ## Tips

- **Plan for the slower cooldown** rather than assuming an evolution increases firing speed.
- **Use its width and knockback** to cover pests approaching on both sides.

## Overview

Ghee Glide is the evolution of [Butter Streak](/weapons/butter-streak). It works the same way, laying a strip of damaging pools along each dash and, when its cooldown is ready, behind Tibo as he walks. The numbers are much higher: 80 damage per hit instead of 28 at Butter Streak's level 8, pools 50% larger (area 1.5), strips lasting 3.5 s, and a 1.8 s cooldown for the walking strip. Pests in the ghee are slowed to half speed for 1 s per hit, as before.

New with the evolution, Tibo moves 15% faster while standing in his own ghee. It is made by opening a chest with Butter Streak at level 8 and [Spud Sneakers](/passives/spud-sneakers) at any level (see [Evolutions](/evolutions) and [Chests](/chests)).

## How it works

- Placement, hit timing (every 0.3 s, one shared re-hit timer per pest across all strips) and the slow are the same as on [Butter Streak](/weapons/butter-streak).
- With area 1.5 each pool has radius 13.5, so a strip is 5 pools along the 48-unit dash path.
- **Speed boost**: while Tibo is inside any zone with a haste value, his move speed is multiplied by `1 + haste`, using the largest haste of the zones he is in. Ghee pools have haste 0.15. <small>[sim-CrpXuTSY.js: `haste[i]=n.trail?.haste??0`]</small> Overlapping strips do not stack the bonus.
- As on Butter Streak, the "burns" in the description is the pool damage itself; there is no burn status in the weapon's data.

## Tips

- Walking along a strip you just laid keeps the 15% speed bonus, which helps when kiting.
- Spud Sneakers is also the catalyst for [Railfry](/weapons/railfry), so one copy can serve both evolutions if you carry [Fry Lance](/weapons/fry-lance) too.

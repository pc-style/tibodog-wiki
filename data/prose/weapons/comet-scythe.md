## How it works

Comet Scythe is always active. Its blades orbit Tibo while their reach breathes in and out: base radius 34 is multiplied by `√Area` and a sinusoidal factor from 0.4 to 1.7. At Area 1, the blade centres range from 13.6 to 57.8 units away. The radius cycle takes two complete angular laps. Extra Amount adds evenly spaced blades, not independent cooldown casts; the base weapon does not counter-swing alternate blades. <small>[sim-B2Iz1j78.js: `` return e.orbitRadius*Math.sqrt(t.area)*Ty(e,n,r) ``]</small> <small>[sim-B2Iz1j78.js: `` let r=(e.reachOut+e.reachIn)/2,i=(e.reachOut-e.reachIn)/2,a=O(t/e.lapsPerSwing) ``]</small>

The collision radius around each blade is `9 × Area`. Overlap checks include the target's body. A target can be hit again after 0.3 s (18 ticks at 60 Hz), **shared across this weapon's blades**; several blades touching the same pest do not each bypass that gate. Angular speed uses raw speed times `1 + 0.5 × (projectile-speed multiplier − 1)`, not the full projectile-speed multiplier. <small>[sim-B2Iz1j78.js: `` return t.raw.speed*(1+.5*(e.loadout.stats.projSpeedMult-1)) ``]</small> <small>[sim-B2Iz1j78.js: `` e.tick-i.hitStamp[a]<u||(i.hitStamp[a]=e.tick,Z(e,r)) ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-DwGb2BE_.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-mechanics-groups.mjs` called the unmodified original swing and collision consumers with fixed targets. It checked both reach extremes and parity, hits at ticks 100/118 but not 117, and an outside-orbit control. This isolates reach and cadence, not natural-run DPS or every rune interaction.

## Overview

Starch Garden unites [Potato Patch](/weapons/potato-patch) and [Spark Corral](/weapons/spark-corral). Own both evolutions and open a [chest](/chests) to combine them and free a weapon slot. Tibo still throws seed potatoes near pests, but walking also plants posts with potatoes at their feet. As those plants ripen, the arcs between their posts grow wider and hit harder.

## How it works

- **Throw seed potatoes.** The thrown plants follow [Seed Potato's rules](/weapons/seed-potato#how-it-works): a pest must be within 90 units, throws are at least 0.25 s × cooldown multiplier apart, and flight takes 0.35 s. Starch Garden allows four thrown plants before Amount bonuses, capped at six. These are separate from the plants on posts. <small>[sim-BIMMSp4H.js: `s=Math.min(n.maxPlants,6,Math.max(1,t.raw.amount)+o)`]</small>
- **Walk to plant posts.** Every 60 units walked creates a post and a stage 1 plant at Tibo's position. Posts last 9 s × Duration, or 11.25 s with Tibo's base +25%. The post limit is four plus extra Amount: seven with raw Amount 4 and Tibo's base +1; GPU's +1 reaches the cap of eight. A new post links to the previous one only when they are 1 to 90 units apart. <small>[sim-BIMMSp4H.js: `u=e.time+Math.max(r.fence.minLife,r.postSec*i.duration)`]</small>
- **Let the plants ripen.** Plants reach stages 2 and 3 after 1.2 and 2.4 growth seconds. Duration speeds growth, so Tibo's base bonus brings those times to 0.96 and 1.92 s. Each plant zaps once per second for 0.3 × damage × stage: 19.5, 39 or 58.5 before bonuses, within 30 × Area units. <small>[sim-BIMMSp4H.js: `q(e,r.damage*n.zapK*a.stage[i],t.src,t.slot,0)`]</small>
- **Grow stronger arcs.** An arc uses the mean stage of its two endpoint plants, minus one. Each step adds 20% arc damage and 25% width. Two stage 1 plants give 65 damage and width 54; two ripe plants give 91 damage and width 81 before bonuses. A stage 1–stage 3 pair gives 78 and 67.5. Area multiplies width. A pest is hit at most once every 0.2 s across this weapon's arcs. <small>[sim-BIMMSp4H.js: `return(Ov(e,t,n-1)+Ov(e,t,n))/2-1`]</small> <small>[sim-BIMMSp4H.js: `q(e,r.damage*(1+n.arcStageK*c),t.src,t.slot,r.knockback)`]</small>
- **Harvest, leave, return.** A post's plant starts unarmed so it is not harvested underfoot when planted. Walk outside its harvest reach, then return within 24 units (pickup bonuses can raise this to 34). Harvests deal 65, 162.5 or 260 before bonuses according to stage, and ripe harvests retain Seed Potato's luck-based gold chance. The post remains, but its plant resets to stage 1 and must be left and approached again before another harvest. Its weaker plant also lowers the connected arcs' strength. <small>[sim-BIMMSp4H.js: `n.state[t]=ja.planted,n.stage[t]=1,n.stageAt[t]=e.time,n.growth[t]=0,n.armed[t]=0`]</small>
- **Posts expire with their plants.** Expiry or replacement at the post cap makes that plant burst for 0.6 × damage (39 before bonuses), then releases it. Post plants do not use the thrown potatoes' separate eight-second timeout. <small>[sim-BIMMSp4H.js: `a.state[o]===ja.planted&&a.slot[o]===t.slot&&(rf(e,t,n,r,o),a.state[o]=ja.hand),aE(i)`]</small>

## Tips

- **Keep ripe arcs between you and pests.** Harvesting gives a burst but resets the plant, trading sustained arc strength for immediate damage.
- **Walk back over old ground.** A new post cannot be harvested immediately under Tibo; leave its reach and return after it ripens.
- **Keep the catalysts.** Fertilizer and GPU are needed to make the two partners and still help the union's growth and post count.

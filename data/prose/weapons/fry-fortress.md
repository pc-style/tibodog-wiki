## Overview

Fry Fortress evolves [Fry Cook Sentry](/weapons/fry-sentry). Reach level 8 with the Sentry, hold [Slow Cooker](/passives/slow-cooker) at any level, then open a [chest](/chests). The evolution keeps stationary turrets, with longer range and piercing bolts.

## How it works

- Base stats are 21 damage, three turrets per drop, a 5 s cooldown and 7 s duration. Tibo's built-in +1 amount makes four turrets before other bonuses. Slow Cooker adds duration on top of Tibo's starting +25%.
- Each turret targets the nearest pest within 180 units and fires every 0.3 s. Bolts have two base pierce and speed 260. The turret stays where it was placed; moving Tibo does not bring it along. <small>[start-rows-Btm4LDau.js: `size:4,range:180`]</small>
- This is not a flat damage increase over level-8 Sentry's 24 base damage: the Fortress trades some damage per bolt for range, firing rate, pierce and uptime.

**Controlled comparison:** original-engine Standard/Normal runs on seeds `s1`, `s2`, `s3` used only the granted selected weapon, refilled HP, 8 s warmup and six high-HP pests held 45–90 units to the right for 20 s, with other spawning culled. Fortress dealt 21,504–23,688 damage. Existing base-weapon controls matched the previous build. This concentrated-target setup is not natural-run DPS. Inserted charged-chest controls evolved level-8 Sentry with Slow Cooker in all three seeds, never without the catalyst or in the old build; they do not establish natural acquisition or chest odds.

## Tips

- **Fight near your turrets.** Their extra reach helps, but they still cannot follow a long kite. Circle back through recent drops rather than abandoning them.
- Hold [Gravy Pack](/weapons/gravy-pack) too, then open a chest to unite both evolutions into [Fry Brigade](/weapons/fry-brigade). The union's following pups plant turrets as they move.

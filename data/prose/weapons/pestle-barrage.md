## Overview

Pestle Barrage evolves [Mash Mortar](/weapons/mash-mortar) with [Big Bowl](/passives/big-bowl). Reach level 8 with Mash Mortar, hold the catalyst and meet the usual [chest evolution gates](/chests). Its explosive globs add three smaller bursts around each main burst.

## How it works

- Raw Amount is 3 mortars per drop, making four with Tibo's built-in +1 before other bonuses. Raw cooldown is 6 s and duration is 8 s. Mortars remain stationary and target pests within 200 units.
- Raw damage is 30, firing interval is 1.3 s and Area is 1.5. The burst radius is 24 × current Area, giving 36 before global Area bonuses, compared with Mash Mortar's 24.
- Each main burst creates three smaller bursts at half damage and 60% of its radius. ## Tips

- **Keep groups near your mortars.** The extra bursts cover an area rather than guaranteeing concentrated damage against one target.
- Big Bowl raises Area as well as enabling the recipe; the raw table below does not include that passive's bonuses.

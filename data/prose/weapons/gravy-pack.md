## How it works

Gravy Pack is Tater Pup's evolution. Its familiars persist beside Tibo instead of expiring after the projectile Duration. They ease toward an elliptical formation with radii 18 by 14, which rotates over time. Effective Amount sets the familiar count: raw 4 plus Tibo's +1 gives five. Lowering Amount removes excess familiars. <small>[sim-D0OxtlYP.js: `` i.life[o]=0,i.t[o]=a*lP ``]</small> <small>[sim-D0OxtlYP.js: `` e.player.x[e.me]+18*D(i)-r.x[t] ``]</small>

Each familiar has its own shooting timer. When ready, it finds the nearest eligible target **within 170 units of the familiar**, not Tibo, and fires a straight projectile. An aiming rune can replace the default nearest-target choice. With no target it retries shortly instead of spending a full shot cooldown. The projectile uses damage 30, speed 230, raw duration 1.5 s and pierce 2 before shared modifiers. The 0.55-second cooldown governs each familiar, not the whole pack taking turns. <small>[sim-D0OxtlYP.js: `` p=Rj(e,s,d,f,c.range) ``]</small> <small>[sim-D0OxtlYP.js: `` if(p===void 0){n.t[o]=AP;continue} ``]</small>

**Every successfully created familiar shot heals 0.05 HP**, capped by Tibo's max HP. It need not hit or kill a pest first. Failed projectile allocation does not reach that heal callback. This is not lifesteal proportional to damage. <small>[sim-D0OxtlYP.js: `` n||t.kind!==`familiar`||t.heal===void 0||qd(e,t.heal) ``]</small>

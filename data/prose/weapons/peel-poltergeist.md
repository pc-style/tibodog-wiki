## Overview

Peel Poltergeist evolves [Peel Phantom](/weapons/peel-phantom). Reach level 8 with Peel Phantom, hold [Peel Out](/passives/peel-out) at any level, then open a [chest](/chests). Each dash leaves a long-lived husk at the dash's starting point. Nearby pests chase it into spinning peels; when the husk expires, it explodes and throws ten peels outward.

## How it works

- The husk lasts 24 s × Duration, or **30 s with Tibo's base +25% Duration**. Its lure reaches 160 × √Area, about 214.7 units at base Area 1.8. Extra Amount adds husks spread sideways across the dash origin; it does not add peels to the expiry ring. <small>[sim-dQVU-VqL.js: `c=Math.max(1,Math.round(r.amount)),l=Ed(n.lure,r.area),u=T(r.duration)`]</small> <small>[sim-dQVU-VqL.js: `let f=(r-(c-1)/2)*n.spread,p=Dd(e,i.dashFromX[a]-s*f,i.dashFromY[a]+o*f,l,u,t.slot)`]</small>
- While the husk is alive, spinning peels check an area every 0.25 s. Each check deals 30% of weapon damage: **36 base damage** within radius 34 × √Area, about 45.6 units. This is an area check around the husk, not four separately colliding projectiles. The four orbiting peels are its visual effect. <small>[sim-dQVU-VqL.js: `i.owner[c]===t.slot&&Od(e,c)&&(i.endsAt[c]-e.tick)%a===0&&(Y(e,r.damage*n.cut,t.src,t.slot,0),zy(e,i.x[c],i.y[c],o))`]</small> <small>[scene-GE3RDRcz.js: `orbitPeels:4,turnsPerSec:1.4,peelLift:7,peelGlow:.7,huskGlow:.4`]</small>
- On expiry, the husk deals **120 base damage** within radius 30 × √Area, about 40.25 units. It then releases a ring of **ten peels**, each dealing 60% of weapon damage (72 base). The ring starts at the spin radius, outside the smaller explosion. <small>[sim-dQVU-VqL.js: `i.owner[s]===t.slot&&kd(e,s)&&(By(e,t,r,s,a),Uy(e,t,n,r,s),Ad(e,s))`]</small> <small>[sim-dQVU-VqL.js: `Vy.gap=n.spin===void 0?0:n.spin.radius*Math.sqrt(r.area)`]</small>
- Ring peels fly straight outward at base speed 190, have Pierce 3 and radius 5 × √Area (about 6.71 at base), and last 1.4 s. Their lifetime is the ring's own value, not the husk's 24 s × Duration. <small>[sim-dQVU-VqL.js: `o.radius[s]=r.size*Math.sqrt(i.area),o.damage[s]=i.damage*r.cut,o.pierce[s]=i.pierce,o.life[s]=r.life,o.maxLife[s]=r.life`]</small> <small>[start-rows-CApiIQX_.js: `ring:{peels:10,cut:.6,size:5,life:1.4}`]</small>

**Controlled tests:** original `main-Df0r1NTs.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`, granted Poltergeist alone, HP refilled, 8 s warmup, then one dash and 36 s with six high-HP original pests held 20–70 units right of the dash origin: 13,500–17,784 damage. Existing Peeler and Peel Phantom level-1/level-8 controls matched the preceding build. Separate original-engine no-target runs, with and without optional combat/area rules, kept the husk alive through tick 1799 and spawned ten 72-damage peels at tick 1800. Original damage-queue controls with stationary zero-radius targets distinguish spin reach 45.5/45.7 and explosion reach 40.1/40.4; wrong-owner, dead and expired husks do not spin. Original single-reward chest controls on both builds and three seeds evolved only the new build's level-8 + Peel Out setup. Grants, held targets, HP refill and direct function/reward calls are fixtures, not natural acquisition, drop odds, practical DPS or server acceptance.

## Tips

- **Leave the husk in the crowd's path.** Dash out of a pack so pursuers gather around the starting point, rather than leaving the husk far from them.
- Duration keeps the lure and spin running longer, but also delays its explosion and ring. Area enlarges the lure, spin, explosion and peel size.

**Spoiler warning.** This page gives away the jokes, scripted lines and character reveals hidden in tibo.dog, including who is inside the amusing suit. If you want to find them yourself, play first.

The developer's launch post says the game has easter eggs (“i hope you enjoy it and find all the easter eggs”, [launch post](https://x.com/poteto/status/2117004709198475365)). This page lists the hidden or easy-to-miss content found in game build 41 (`main-B2gf34bA.js`) outside the data tables: scripted lines, cutscenes and jokes. Mechanics behind the visitors are on [Visitors](/visitors) and [Story mode](/story-mode).

## Experimental update2 playtest

**This is an opt-in playtest, not ordinary Open World balance.** `?update2` or `?update2=1` selects all seven parts (`circlet`, `boots`, `medley`, `pylons`, `rift`, `stockpot`, `content`) and now defaults to the **Dig** endgame loop. `?update2=0` disables the playtest. A comma-separated list keeps recognized parts; `?update2=pylons,rift` selects those parts without an endgame loop. Add `dig`, `heat` or `bounty` to choose a loop, for example `?update2=pylons,heat`; if several loops are named, the first wins. A loop on its own enables all seven parts. If no part or loop is recognized, the parser enables all seven parts plus Dig. The simulation accepts playtest options only in Standard without a Daily; Story and Daily do not activate them. <small>[language-ahead-BgcRnx9P.js: ``H=[`circlet`,`boots`,`medley`,`pylons`,`rift`,`stockpot`,`content`],U=[`dig`,`heat`,`bounty`]``]</small> <small>[language-ahead-BgcRnx9P.js: ``let t=e.split(`,`),n=t.filter(W),r=t.find(G)??(n.length===0?`dig`:void 0)``]</small> <small>[sim-CCvdOWKf.js: ``if(t===void 0||e.mode!==`standard`||e.daily!==void 0)return!1``]</small>

**Endgame loop playtest (build 240).** These loops begin at the configured survival boundary (15:00 in current Standard), not when the URL is opened. They are separate from ordinary endgame phases and do not add weapons, enemies or POIs to the ordinary catalogs. The start card adds the selected loop's rule; the playtest HUD offers its progress/deadline row through the existing row cap, and free-reward panels use loop-specific titles rather than the paid Spud Forge prompt. <small>[sim-CCvdOWKf.js: ``function $_(e){return e.runClock>=e.cfg.win.timeSec}``]</small> <small>[endgame-text-DwxVJMRR.js: ``message:[`Turn up the heat?`]``]</small> <small>[endgame-text-DwxVJMRR.js: ``message:[`Bounty reward`]``]</small>

- **Dig:** ordinary kills add 1 fill and elites add 40, only after the survival boundary and while no Dig boss is active. The first requirement is 500; later requirements are `round(500 × (1 + 0.6 × (level − 1)))`. Filling the meter calls the next boss in a fixed eleven-boss cycle, beginning with The Masher. Its deadline is 60 run-clock seconds. If its pool identity disappears before timeout, the loop advances a level, resets fill, offers a free eligible limit break, and sets future HP/damage scaling multipliers to `1.2^(level − 1)` / `1.1^(level − 1)`. Timeout removes that boss, keeps half the required fill and does not advance the level. Some ordinary scheduled arrivals are suppressed while Dig is selected; this is not a claim that every existing boss or event is removed. <small>[intro-shared-z1Z_MtsO.js: ``need:500,needGrowth:.6,eliteKills:40,bossSec:60,failKeep:.5,hpPerLevel:1.2,damagePerLevel:1.1``]</small> <small>[sim-CCvdOWKf.js: ``n.fill+=t.elite?Ft.eliteKills:1``]</small> <small>[sim-CCvdOWKf.js: ``t.bossesCalled%Tn.length``]</small>
- **Heat:** the first free limit-break offer is due 10 seconds after the survival boundary, then every 60 seconds. Accepting increases Heat by one and sets the scaling multipliers to `1.3^Heat` HP and `1.2^Heat` damage; leaving the offer without increasing the run's break count records a decline without increasing Heat. These are inputs to future scaling calculations, not proof that already spawned pests gain that much HP immediately. With no eligible equipment there is no reward card. <small>[intro-shared-z1Z_MtsO.js: ``firstSec:10,everySec:60,hpPerLevel:1.3,damagePerLevel:1.2``]</small> <small>[sim-CCvdOWKf.js: ``e.limitBreak.count<=t.countAt?t.declined++``]</small>
- **Bounty:** the first champion attempt is due 10 seconds after the survival boundary, then 40 seconds after an attempt. It spawns 220 units away, with up to eight directions checked against props, and flees at 30 units/s under its dedicated movement. Its HP is at least the spawned champion HP and the recent damage-rate estimate × 1 second; the spawn requests a ×2 HP multiplier. The 25-second deadline uses the run clock. A missing target identity counts as caught, increasing the streak and paying **300 × streak gold directly**; every second catch offers a free eligible limit break. Escape removes the target and resets the streak. A still-active target delays another attempt; these are scheduling rules, not guaranteed natural encounters. <small>[intro-shared-z1Z_MtsO.js: ``firstSec:10,everySec:40,escapeSec:25,spawnDistance:220,fleeSpeed:30,hpMult:2,healthSec:1``]</small> <small>[sim-CCvdOWKf.js: ``let n=wt.goldPerStreak*t.streak;e.loadout.gold+=n,e.run.goldEarned+=n``]</small> <small>[sim-CCvdOWKf.js: ``t.streak%wt.breakEvery===0&&nv(e,`bounty`)``]</small>

## How it works

Crab Tide surrounds Tibo with Sand Crabs at radius 180. Its **raw count is 30**, but the actual request is `max(1, round(30 × difficulty wallPests))`, subject to room in the enemy pool. Normal's multiplier is 0.3, giving nine crabs in an empty supplied arena. They are evenly spaced around the circle, not a single line or a random swarm. <small>[sim-BF7yetWo.js: `` o=Math.max(1,Math.round(t.count*e.cfg.level.wallPests)) ``]</small> <small>[sim-BF7yetWo.js: `` let c=s/o*Math.PI*2 ``]</small>

Every crab walks toward **the position Tibo occupied when the event started**. Moving away does not retarget the ring. The wall consumer stops a crab once it is strictly less than 20 units from that fixed point; exactly 20 still moves. The event assigns the crab's catalog speed instead of the ordinary scaled spawn speed, but it still uses normal enemy/contact systems. Surviving crabs have a 20-second despawn deadline. <small>[sim-BF7yetWo.js: `` r.tx[l]=i,r.ty[l]=a,r.despawnAt[l]=e.time+t.life ``]</small> <small>[sim-BF7yetWo.js: `` o=a<20?0:n.speed[t] ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-DwGb2BE_.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-mechanics-groups.mjs` invoked the original event consumer, checked nine equally spaced radius-180 spawns and their deadlines, moved Tibo to distinguish fixed-centre movement from chasing, and checked both sides of the stopping boundary. This does not measure natural event frequency or survival difficulty.

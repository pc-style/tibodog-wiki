## Overview

Fertilizer makes every XP gem worth more. Each level adds 0.08 XP gain, so gems give ×1.08 XP at level 1 and ×1.40 at level 5. More XP means more level-ups, and each level-up is another weapon or passive card (see [Level-up](/level-up)).

It is the catalyst for three evolutions: [Big Fry](/weapons/big-fry) (from [Small Fry](/weapons/small-fry)), [Overgrowth](/weapons/overgrowth) (from [Seeker Sprouts](/weapons/seeker-sprouts)) and [Potato Patch](/weapons/potato-patch) (from [Seed Potato](/weapons/seed-potato)).

## How it works

Picking up an XP gem adds its value times Tibo's XP gain multiplier.<small>[sim-2ckLPdq7.js: `o===C.xp)e.gemCount--,e.counters.gems++,Tu(e,a*i.growthMult)`]</small> That multiplier is `(1 + XP gain) × (1 + 0.5 × curse) × difficulty XP` (see [Stats](/stats)). The three parts multiply, so Fertilizer at level 5 together with [Rotten Spud](/passives/rotten-spud) at level 5 (curse 0.5) gives 1.4 × 1.25 = ×1.75 before the difficulty factor.

Other XP gain sources are [Compost Tea](/items/compost-tea) (+0.06) and [Spud Shrine](/points-of-interest#spud-shrine) blessings. These add to Fertilizer's total.

## Tips

- **Take it early in a run.** XP gain pays off over the rest of the run, so it is worth more the earlier you take it. Late in a run, when most of your weapons and passives are already maxed, extra levels matter less.
- Fertilizer works well with [Potato Magnet](/passives/potato-magnet), which brings gems in from farther away.

## Overview

Salt Crystal is a common item that adds 0.04 damage (`might`). Copies stack additively. It is one of eight Common items of equal weight, so with no luck it is about 7.4% of random item rolls.

Damage bonuses are summed and then applied as one multiplier, `1 + total` (see [Stats](/stats#stat-conversion)). One crystal is +4 percentage points, added to sources such as [Butter Pat](/passives/butter-pat) (+0.1 per level). It raises weapon damage and item effects that use the damage multiplier, such as [Porcupine Spud](/items/porcupine-spud) fries. Effects that copy a share of a weapon hit, such as [Spicy Croquette](/items/spicy-croquette) blasts, grow with it because the hit itself is bigger.

## Tips

- **Stack copies when they turn up.** One crystal is a small boost. It is a Common, so several can turn up in one run, and they keep adding 4 points each.
- [Thorny Jacket](/items/thorny-jacket) contact damage is flat and is not raised by damage bonuses.

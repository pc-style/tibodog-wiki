## Overview

Lightning Spud can add an explosion to a damaging weapon hit. One stack has a 6% chance per eligible hit, subject to a refilling proc budget. "Two per second" describes that budget's refill, not two guaranteed explosions or a fixed one-second counter.

## How it works

- An eligible hit comes from a weapon slot 0–6 and has positive input damage. The hit resolver calls on-hit items after resolving the weapon hit. Item damage uses slot −1, so Lightning Spud's own explosion cannot trigger another Lightning Spud explosion. Zero-input-damage hits do not qualify. <small>[sim-B7Rtlarz.js: ``h=m>=0&&m<7&&l>0``]</small> <small>[sim-B7Rtlarz.js: ``h&&e.itemFx.hit.length>0&&(s&qe.BossShare)===0``]</small>
- With `n` stacks, chance is `1 − 0.94^n`, and both the refill rate and bucket capacity are `2 × n`. One stack starts with two tokens. An attempted proc first needs at least one token, then a successful random roll; only success spends a token. Failed chance rolls leave the budget intact. Three stacks give a 16.9416% chance and a six-token budget, not 18% and not six guaranteed procs. <small>[start-rows-PTlNrV5v.js: ``effect:{kind:`onHit`,chance:.06,perSec:2,proc:{kind:`blast`,radius:34,share:.9}}``]</small> <small>[sim-B7Rtlarz.js: ``return 1-Ee(1-Math.min(1,Math.max(0,e)),t)``]</small> <small>[sim-B7Rtlarz.js: ``e.rate=r.perSec*t,n&&(e.tokens=e.rate)``]</small> <small>[sim-B7Rtlarz.js: ``return!(t.tokens<1||t.chance<1&&e.rng.next()>=t.chance||(--t.tokens,0))``]</small>
- Tokens refill continuously during game ticks, capped at the bucket's rate. At one stack the nominal refill time is 30 ticks (0.5 s at 60 Hz). In the tested empty-bucket sequence, floating-point addition leaves the budget at 0.9999999999999999 after tick 30, so the first usable token arrives at tick 31. Stored tokens allow an immediate two-proc burst, followed by further successes as refill permits. There is no fixed-window reset or guaranteed sustained proc rate; eligible hits and random rolls still matter. Adding stacks raises capacity but does not itself top up an already initialized bucket. <small>[sim-B7Rtlarz.js: ``n.rate>0&&(n.tokens=Math.min(n.rate,n.tokens+n.rate*t))``]</small>
- The blast is centered on the struck target, in radius `34 × sqrt(Area multiplier)`, with target-body overlap included. Its input damage is 90% of the resolved triggering hit, before the splash hit's own damage processing. It can hit the original victim as well as neighbors. It is not 90% of the weapon's raw stat-table damage, nor a promise that every final damage number will be identical against different defenses and modifiers. <small>[sim-B7Rtlarz.js: ``let o=n.radius*qg(e);Y(e,a*n.share,t.src,-1,.5),n_(e,t,r.x[i],r.y[i],o)``]</small> <small>[sim-B7Rtlarz.js: ``function qg(e){return Math.sqrt(e.loadout.stats.areaMult)}``]</small>

## Controlled checks

Controlled original-engine checks passed on build 206 (`main-CrJIatJG.js`), Standard/Normal, seeds `s1`, `s2`, `s3`, using `node tools/probe-targeting-lures.mjs main-CrJIatJG.js`. Six Lightning Spud scenario groups checked the chance boundary, failed-roll token preservation, two immediate successes followed by a blocked third, 29/30/31-tick refill, capped idle refill, and the three-stack chance/rate. Original hit-resolver fixtures confirmed slot −1 and zero-damage controls, 90 damage to a neighbor from a resolved 100-damage weapon hit, splash on the original victim, inclusive blast-body overlap and no recursive proc.

Separate 20-second full Game runs with unmodified seeded RNG and two inserted high-HP aphids recorded Lightning Spud damage of **42.73 / 148.24 / 55.31** for `s1` / `s2` / `s3`. These are fixed-target wiring checks, not a representative DPS sample or an estimate of the natural 6% proc chance. The other fixtures deliberately force successful rolls to isolate the budget and damage rules. The script grants Peeler and one or three item stacks, holds Peeler's cooldown during isolated controls, and uses no gear, clock warp or HP refill. It does not establish natural drop odds, server behavior, every modifier interaction or practical build strength. See the revision-bound [coverage inventory](/coverage#mechanics-inventory).

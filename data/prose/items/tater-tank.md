## Extra dash

Tater Tank adds **one dash charge**, so an otherwise unmodified Tibo can dash twice before waiting for recharge. It does not shorten the cooldown or increase dash distance. It has a one-stack limit. <small>[boot-92Q5q8IP.js: `` id:`tater-tank`,tier:`base`,rarity:2,mods:{dashCharges:1},maxStacks:1,weight:5 ``]</small>

**Save the second dash for the way out.** One charge can carry you through a gap; the other lets you leave if pests close around the landing spot. [Butter Socks](/passives/butter-socks) reduces recharge time separately.

**Measured** in `main-dPufllLj.js`, Standard/Normal and Story/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: after one second of initialization, a granted stack and six seconds to recharge, three dash attempts 0.3 seconds apart produced two dashes. Without the item, both this build and the preceding build produced one. HP was refilled by the harness. These controlled grants establish the effect, not natural acquisition, drop odds or server acceptance.

## Overview

Compost Tea is an Uncommon item that adds 6% XP gain per copy. It adds to the same growth stat as [Fertilizer](/passives/fertilizer) (8% per level). The total is then multiplied by curse's XP bonus and the difficulty's XP multiplier ([Stats](/stats#stat-conversion)). More XP means more level-ups, and so more weapon and passive picks over a run ([Level-up](/level-up)).

It has weight 8 out of 78 among Uncommon items, about 2.6% of item rolls at luck 0. Copies stack with no cap. It has no evolution.

## Tips

- XP gain pays off over time. A copy found early in a run is worth more than one found near the end.

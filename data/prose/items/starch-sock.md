## Overview

Starch Sock is a common item that adds 0.04 move speed. Copies stack additively. It is one of eight Common items of equal weight, so with no luck it is about 7.4% of random item rolls.

Move speed is summed and applied as `1 + total` to Tibo's base speed of 62 (see [Stats](/stats#stat-conversion)). One sock adds about 2.5 units per second. [Spud Sneakers](/passives/spud-sneakers) gives +0.08 per level, twice as much.

## Tips

- Extra speed helps you get around enemies, reach points of interest and outrun slow pests. Its effect is small unless you have several socks or Spud Sneakers as well.

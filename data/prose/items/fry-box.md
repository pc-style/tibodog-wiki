## Overview

Fry Box is a Rare item that gives Shot-tag weapons one extra projectile per copy, up to two copies. It stacks with the extra projectiles from [Four-Eyed Spud](/passives/four-eyed-spud), which grants +1 at level 2 and +2 total at level 4. <small>[boot-DRcM3mn0.js: ``id:`fry-box`,tier:`base`,rarity:2,mods:{},maxStacks:2,weight:6``]</small>

## How it works

This is a tag-specific bonus, not the global amount stat from [Extra Eye](/items/extra-eye). The Shot tag includes Frozen Fries, Grab Bag, Small Fry, Tot Toss, Fry Knives, Hot Potato and Seeker Sprouts, their Shot-tag evolutions and other Shot-tag weapons. Fry Lance is Storm and Spud Dice is Blast, so neither gets extra shots from Fry Box. <small>[sim-B8UwV9Dw.js: ``"frozen-fries":`shot`,"grab-bag":`shot`,"small-fry":`shot`,"tot-toss":`shot`,"fry-knives":`shot`,"hot-potato":`shot`,"seeker-sprouts":`shot` ``]</small>

Each copy adds one to the Shot bonus; two boxes and level-4 Four-Eyed Spud give +4 together. <small>[sim-B8UwV9Dw.js: `tags:n.tags,amount:n.amount*r`]</small> Once you hold two, Fry Box stops appearing in ordinary item rolls. It has no evolution.

Controlled original-engine comparison (seed `projectile-boundary`, Normal standard mode; weapons, passive levels and items granted directly) checks the levels and stacking separately from natural acquisition. These setups do not measure drop odds.

## Tips

- **Check the Shot tag, not the weapon's appearance.** A weapon can fire projectiles without belonging to Shot. Fry Box is most useful when several of your weapons share that tag.

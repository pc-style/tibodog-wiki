## Overview

Golden Gravy is an Epic item that adds 15% damage and 15% gold gain per copy. The damage bonus adds to the same stat as [Butter Pat](/passives/butter-pat), and the gold bonus to the same stat as [Golden Fry](/passives/golden-fry). Gold gain is then multiplied by curse's gold bonus ([Stats](/stats#stat-conversion)).

It has weight 3 out of 9 among Epic items, about 1.3% of item rolls at luck 0. Copies stack with no cap. It has no evolution.

## Tips

- **Take it for gold and damage.** Gold buys [Potato Crates](/points-of-interest#potato-crate), [Fry Cart](/points-of-interest#fry-cart) items and [Tater Slots](/points-of-interest#tater-slots) spins, which all give more items. Golden Gravy speeds that up while also raising damage.
- It pairs well with [Coupon Book](/items/coupon-book): one item increases the gold you earn and the other reduces what you spend.

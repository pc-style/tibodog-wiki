## Overview

Copper Spoon is a Common item that adds 10% pickup range per copy. Base pickup range is 40 units, so each spoon adds 4 units. XP gems and coins inside the range fly to Tibo. Chests and items never do, so Tibo still has to walk over those ([Pickups](/pickups)). Spoons stack additively with [Potato Magnet](/passives/potato-magnet), which gives 25% per level.

It has weight 10 out of 80 among Common items. Common is the most likely rarity, so this is one of the items you will see most often: about 7.4% of item rolls at luck 0. It has no evolution.

## Tips

- A bigger pickup range lets Tibo collect XP without walking into the crowd. It is a comfort pick rather than a power pick.

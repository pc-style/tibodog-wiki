## Overview

Lucky Peel is a common stat item that adds 0.05 luck. Copies stack: each one adds another 0.05. It is one of eight Common items, all with the same weight, so with no luck it is about 7.4% of random item rolls (59% Common × 10/80, computed from the rarity and item weights).

Luck does three things (see [Stats](/stats#luck)): it gives a chance of a fourth level-up card equal to your luck, it raises the 3- and 5-reward chances of [chests](/chests), and it multiplies the weight of rarity *n* items by `(1 + luck)^n`. One peel therefore means a 5% chance of a fourth card. On item rolls it raises the Legendary weight by about 22% (1.05⁴).

Lucky Peel is also the game's fallback item. If an item roll finds nothing it is allowed to give, you get a Lucky Peel. <small>[sim-CGcyGQGp.js: `` ??d.item(`lucky-peel`) ``]</small>

## Tips

- One peel changes little. Several, or peels on top of [Four-Eyed Spud](/passives/four-eyed-spud) (+0.1 luck per level) or [Cursed Crown](/items/cursed-crown), add up to a fourth card more often.
- Luck only improves item rarity on future rolls. It is most worth having early, before you buy [Potato Crates](/points-of-interest#potato-crate) or visit Fry Carts.

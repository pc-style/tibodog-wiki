## Overview

Panic Fry is a Rare item that gives Tibo a short damage window when his HP crosses from at least 50% to strictly below 50%. While it is active, ordinary hits deal 25% more damage, pests move at 95% speed and pest-owned bullets travel at 95% speed. Boss movement and boss-owned bullets are not slowed.

## How it works

- **Cross the threshold; do not just stay low.** Exactly 50% HP does not trigger it. Acquiring the item while already below half does not trigger it either: Tibo must first recover to at least half, then fall below again. The engine remembers whether he was below on the previous tick. <small>[sim-DUXHKfs3.js: `i=n>0&&r<n*Lt.hpShare,a=MC(e),o=function(e){return AC(e)?It:Lt}(e),s=Math.floor(e.runClock/o.bucketSec)`]</small> <small>[sim-DUXHKfs3.js: `if(a>0&&i&&!t.below&&t.bucket!==s)`]</small>
- **One activation per run-clock minute.** The allowance resets at 1:00, 2:00 and so on, not 60 seconds after the last activation. A crossing at 0:59.8 and another at 1:00 can both work if Tibo recovers to half in between. Remaining below half across that boundary does not activate it again.
- **Copies extend the window, not its strength.** One through five copies give 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 seconds. Extra copies beyond the five-copy definition cap do not extend it further. Duration bonuses do not multiply this window. The effect ends at its expiry time, rather than one tick after it. <small>[sim-DUXHKfs3.js: `r=o.windowSec+(n-1)*o.perStackSec`]</small> <small>[sim-DUXHKfs3.js: `e.runClock>=t.from&&e.runClock<t.until`]</small>
- **Damage applies when the hit resolves.** The ×1.25 multiplier applies to ordinary hits against pests, elites and bosses, including non-weapon sources. It is separate from Tibo's Might stat. Instant-kill and special boss-HP-share hits bypass this ordinary-damage branch. <small>[sim-DUXHKfs3.js: `e.moreVariety!==void 0&&(l*=ac().hitMult(e))`]</small>
- **The slow is slight, not a freeze.** It reduces ordinary pest movement by 5% across the map. An existing stronger movement slow wins instead of adding or multiplying the two slows: [Mashquake](/weapons/mashquake)'s 40% slow and [Glacier Spud](/items/glacier-spud)'s 50% slow stay stronger. Bosses keep their existing status slow, without Panic Fry's extra effect. <small>[sim-DUXHKfs3.js: `let i=t===e.enemies?r:0`]</small> <small>[sim-DUXHKfs3.js: `let u=t.slowT[a]>0?t.slowF[a]:0,d=1-(u>i?u:i)`]</small>
- **Bullet ownership matters.** Pest-owned hostile bullets slow by 5%; boss-owned bullets do not. Tibo's friendly projectiles use a separate pool and keep their speed. This is a movement reduction, not bullet removal or extra projectile lifetime. <small>[sim-DUXHKfs3.js: `return!NC(e)||MC(e)<=0||t>=ku?1:1-Lt.slowFactor`]</small>

**Earlier-Daily compatibility:** Daily days before 20780 (23 November 2026 UTC) instead use fixed **45-second buckets**, with **2.25 / 3 / 3.75 / 4.5 / 5.25-second** windows for one through five copies. Ordinary runs and Dailies from that date keep the minute buckets and 3–7-second windows above. The shorter general wave timeline does not shorten Panic Fry everywhere. <small>[sim-DUXHKfs3.js: `function AC(e){return e.daily!==void 0&&e.daily.day<e.cfg.daily.fifteenMinuteDay}`]</small>

## Tips

- **Use the damage window to clear an escape route.** The 5% slow is small; it is not a substitute for healing or a stronger crowd-control source. This is positioning advice, not a measured survival benefit.
- **Heal back to half to rearm the crossing.** Healing does not cancel an active window, but another drop in the same run-clock minute cannot start a new one.

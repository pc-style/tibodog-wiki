## Overview

Cursed Crown is a Legendary item that trades danger for power. Each copy gives +30% damage, +0.2 luck and +0.25 curse.

- **Damage**: adds to the same stat as [Butter Pat](/passives/butter-pat), multiplying all weapon damage.
- **Luck**: gives a 20% chance of a fourth level-up card, slightly bigger chests and better item rarity rolls ([Stats](/stats#luck)).
- **Curse**: multiplies pest, elite and boss HP and spawn rates by 1.25 and pest speed by 1.075. It also raises XP and gold gain by 12.5% ([Curse](/curse)).

Legendary items share the smallest rarity weight, and Cursed Crown has weight 1 out of 4 among them. At luck 0 that is about 0.5% of item rolls. On difficulties without revives, where [Second Spud](/items/second-spud) is removed from the pool, it is 1 out of 3. Copies stack with no cap. It has no evolution.

## How it works

The crown's curse is the curse stat only, like [Rotten Spud](/passives/rotten-spud)'s. It does not count as a curse taken, so unlike accepting a [Moldy Shrine](/points-of-interest#moldy-shrine), it never adds extra elites or bosses.

## Tips

- +30% damage equals three levels of [Butter Pat](/passives/butter-pat) and takes no passive slot. Whether the extra enemy HP is worth it depends on how comfortably your build is clearing.
- Curse makes kill-based item effects that scale with enemy max HP, such as [Kaboom Spud](/items/kaboom-spud), [Freezer Burn](/items/freezer-burn) and [Glacier Spud](/items/glacier-spud), hit harder, because those enemies have more HP.

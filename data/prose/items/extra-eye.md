## Overview

Extra Eye is an Epic item that adds one projectile to Tibo's weapons per copy. You can hold at most 3 copies. Once you have three it stops appearing in item rolls, and the Microwave cannot copy it. <small>[sim-CRdlxLJj.js: `(t.maxStacks===void 0||e.loadout.itemCount(t.id)<t.maxStacks)`]</small>

It has weight 3 out of 9 among Epic items, so at luck 0 about 1.3% of item rolls land on it. It has no evolution.

## How it works

The bonus adds to the same amount stat as [Sprouting Eyes](/passives/sprouting-eyes) and [GPU](/passives/gpu). The amount stat counts only whole points, so Extra Eye's +1 always gives a full extra projectile. It applies to every weapon except the `aura`, `cone`, `nuke` and `ring` kinds: [Steam Vent](/weapons/steam-vent), [Deep Fryer](/weapons/deep-fryer), [Reset Button](/weapons/reset-button), [Root Cellar](/weapons/root-cellar) and their evolutions. <small>[sim-CRdlxLJj.js: `` return e!==`aura`&&e!==`cone`&&e!==`nuke`&&e!==`ring` ``]</small>

## Tips

- **Take it for one- or two-projectile weapons.** An extra projectile is a large boost for weapons that fire one or two projectiles per volley, and it applies to every eligible weapon at once.
- If your main damage comes from the excluded weapon kinds, an Extra Eye does little for them.

## Overview

The Garden Slug is the tier-2 pest of [Mash Swamp](/biomes/mash-swamp), and the only one, so every tier-2 spawn in the swamp is a slug. Tier-2 pests trickle in from the first minute, peak around 5:00 and stop spawning at 12:00 on Normal (13:00 on Easy). Between 1:00 and 4:59 the minute's [elite](/bosses#elites) is a Garden Slug if Tibo is in the swamp.

Its behaviour is listed as `tank`, but that label does not change how it moves: the game treats `tank` exactly like `chase`, so the slug just crawls straight at Tibo and hurts on contact. <small>[sim-BJSyZ192.js: `` case`chase`:case`tank`:case`splitter`:return; ``]</small> What makes it a tank is its stats. Its 34 base HP is the most of any tier-2 pest, it is larger than most (radius 7), and its mass of 0.4 cuts knockback by 40%. In exchange it is very slow, at speed 22, the slowest pest outside events apart from the [Reset Zombie](/enemies/reset-zombie). It deals 7 contact damage. A kill gives 2 XP and a 6% chance of a coin.

## Tips

- **Keep moving and avoid getting boxed in.** At about a third of Tibo's base speed, slugs pose little threat to a Tibo who keeps moving. The danger is getting boxed in by a slow crowd that takes a long time to clear.
- With [Bog Slimes](/enemies/bog-slime) and Ketchup Drops at tier 1, Mash Swamp's early game is slow and tanky: lots of HP to chew through, but little that can catch Tibo.

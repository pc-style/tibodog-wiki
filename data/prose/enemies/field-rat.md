## Overview

The Field Rat is the most widespread tier-3 pest. It is in the tier-3 pool of six biomes: [Peel Forest](/biomes/peel-forest), [Compost Caves](/biomes/compost-caves), [Coin Laundromat](/biomes/coin-laundromat), [Frozen-Fries Tundra](/biomes/frozen-tundra), the [Fry Palace Drive-Thru](/biomes/fry-palace) and [Gutterball Lanes](/biomes/gutterball-lanes). On Normal, tier-3 pests spawn from 6:00 to 16:59 (from 7:00 on Easy), and between 5:00 and 9:59 a Field Rat can be the minute's [elite](/bosses#elites). Rats also keep appearing after that window, because every [Harvest Mouse](/enemies/harvest-mouse) splits into three of them when it dies.

It is a plain chaser that runs straight at Tibo and hurts only on contact, but it is fast. Its speed of 50 is the highest of any tier-3 pest and only 12 below Tibo's [base move speed](/stats#player-base-values) of 62. It has 42 base HP and deals 11 damage. Its mass of 0.2 trims knockback by 20%. A kill gives 4 XP and an 8% chance of a coin.

Rats that come out of a Harvest Mouse are full Field Rats, scaled to match the mouse they came from. See the [Harvest Mouse](/enemies/harvest-mouse) page for details.

## Tips

- **Kill rats instead of outrunning them.** Outrunning rats takes a long time: at base speed Tibo gains only 12 units per second on them, and less once [curse](/curse) or overtime speeds pests up. Killing them is usually better than trying to kite.
- When a Harvest Mouse dies, its three rats start right on top of where it fell, already close to Tibo if it was killed up close. Killing mice at range gives you time to deal with the rats.

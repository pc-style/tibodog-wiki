## How it works

This is a **crow murder member**, not an independent always-chasing pest. It first approaches a slot around Tibo at radius 90. A pack without a surviving alpha closes in; a pack with an alpha holds its formation and chooses lungers. Members entering close mode receive an initial 0–0.35 s stagger. A pack-wide crouch guard and a 0.3-second planned-leap separation gate prevent unrestricted simultaneous lunges; nearby members on sufficiently separated sides can join a volley. <small>[endgame-sim-DgqCMmUS.js: `` Math.abs(Jn(h-Wn[e]))<1.2 ``]</small>

When the relevant pack/individual timers permit, a target **at or inside 100 units** starts a stationary 0.6-second Normal crouch. Aim is fixed then, with a lead from Tibo's velocity (sampled and capped at 70 units/s), not continuously homed during flight. The leap's distance is the predicted target distance plus 20, clamped between 90 and 120; its speed is **220 units/s**, so 120 is a maximum travel distance, not the trigger range. <small>[endgame-sim-DgqCMmUS.js: `` c.leapLen[n]=_e(f+20,90,i.distance),c.leapSpeed[n]=i.speed ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-DgqCMmUS.js: `` c>70&&(a*=70/c,o*=70/c) ``]</small>

During the leap it is **invincible** and contact damage switches to its scaled lunge damage (raw 12, versus ordinary raw contact 1). At the end it loses invincibility, restores ordinary contact damage and stops for 0.6 s of recovery. A member gets a further random 3.6–5.4-second individual lunge delay after recovery; pack timing can postpone it more. A recognised solid prop in the next movement step interrupts a leap and uses a separate **0.8-second recovery**. This does not mean every decorative object or every obstruction blocks it. <small>[endgame-sim-DgqCMmUS.js: `` r.damage[o]=i.leapDmg[o],r.set(o,a.Invincible) ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-DgqCMmUS.js: `` ir(e,n,r)}(t,n,r,M.rockStunSec) ``]</small>

Killing an alpha scatters its surviving pack: members initially flee for 1.5–2 s at 55% speed, then may circle at 25% speed before resuming chase/lunge. The return is staggered by member order (0.45 s per earlier member), not a permanent fear effect. A slot-generation check prevents a replacement enemy from inheriting a dead pack member's role. <small>[endgame-sim-DgqCMmUS.js: `` n.chaseAt[r]=n.fleeUntil[r]+.45*n.order[a] ``]</small> <small>[titan.hot-Dv8uxnY2.js: `` e.memberId[n]!==t.enemies.pool.id(n)?v.none:e.role[n] ``]</small>

The catalog HP/speed is not the whole Hunt encounter. The actual Hunt release spawner applies a speed floor of 1.1 × Tibo's configured walking speed × movement multiplier and replaces member HP with a difficulty/time/ladder-scaled Hunt budget. Alpha HP also depends on leader versus elite status and curse. These spawn overrides do not change the lunge's listed speed. <small>[endgame-sim-DgqCMmUS.js: `` r.speed[o]=Math.max(r.speed[o]??0,i) ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-DgqCMmUS.js: `` tt(n,e,o)===S.member&&(r.maxHp[o]=Cr(e),r.hp[o]=r.maxHp[o]??0) ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-8zbhOvtE.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-hunt-packs.mjs` uses original pack spawners and steering with supplied roles, clocks, attack timers and positions. It checks outside/equal trigger range, crouch deadline, moved-player locked aim, invincibility/damage restoration, recovery/retry and a supplied boulder interruption. Alpha-loss/flee controls cover rat, crow and raccoon packs; beetles have no alpha. These are client-rule fixtures, not naturally reached encounters, survival/DPS, reward/server/device or Nightmare verification. Natural formations, cooperative volley selection, phase spawn scaling and complete obstacle routing remain source-audited rather than measured here.

## How it works

This is a **Shell beast**, not the Court version of this species. It chases until Tibo is strictly inside **90 units** and its attack timer is ready. It then stops for a 0.5-second Normal windup, locks the direction toward Tibo, and charges at **140 units/s** for about **0.836 s**. Moving sideways after the lock does not turn the charge. The nominal charge travel is 1.3 times the trigger range (117 units); it is not the trigger range itself. After charging, it returns to chasing with a 1.6-second attack retry timer. <small>[sim-DfBcZSG3.js: `` if(s>=n||o>0)return; ``]</small> <small>[sim-DfBcZSG3.js: `` i.timer[t]=1.3*n/r ``]</small> <small>[sim-DfBcZSG3.js: `` i.state[t]=0,i.timer[t]=1.6 ``]</small>

While registered and protected in Shell, it takes **10% of ordinary incoming damage**. The attack-state transition from charging back to chasing opens a **1.2-second full-damage window**, then armour returns; exactly at the closing deadline it is protected again. This is not permanent damage resistance attached to any enemy with the same catalog definition. <small>[shell-DoVvjvtq.js: `` protectedDamage:.1,beastOpeningSec:1.2 ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-DdAOcn6h.js: `` a===2&&o===0&&(t.openUntil[i]=e.runClock+Pt(e).beastOpeningSec,t.stats.openings++) ``]</small>

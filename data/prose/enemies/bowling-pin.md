## Overview

The Bowling Pin is a tier-1 pest found only in [Gutterball Lanes](/biomes/gutterball-lanes), the bowling-alley landmark, where it shares the tier-1 slot with the [Crumb Ant](/enemies/crumb-ant). Because the lanes are a landmark, Tibo usually meets pins only after walking into one. On Normal, tier-1 pests make up 8 in 9 spawns until 4:00 and stop spawning at 9:00 (10:00 on Easy), so pins are mostly an early-run sight. As a tier-1 pest it is never an [elite](/bosses#elites).

It shuffles straight at Tibo at speed 30 and hurts only on contact, for 4 base damage. With 10 base HP it is the second-toughest tier-1 pest after the [Bog Slime](/enemies/bog-slime), and its mass of 0.3 is the highest of any tier-1 pest, cutting knockback by 30%. <small>[sim-34F7B_ih.js: `d=o*e.loadout.stats.knockbackMult*(1-s)*70`]</small> A kill gives 1 XP and a 3% chance of a coin.

## Tips

- Pins come mixed with Crumb Ants, which are faster (46) but have only 6 HP. The ants reach Tibo first, and the pins arrive later as a slower second line.
- Knockback weapons push pins less far than the ants beside them, so expect pins to bunch up close to Tibo.

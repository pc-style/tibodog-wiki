## How it works

Fuse Totem is a stationary Shell hazard. Its phase spawner starts a **six-second fuse** and sets speed to zero; the catalog `tank` behavior is not a walking attack. At the fuse deadline it explodes once, then removes itself. Destroying the registered totem before that deadline cancels the explosion; a reused pool slot does not inherit its fuse. <small>[shell-DR4ZVVe_.js: `` n.fuseUntil[t]=e.runClock+X(e).fuseSec,r.speed[t]=0 ``]</small>

The blast checks Tibo’s **centre within radius 180, including equality**, not his body radius. It attempts the totem’s scaled damage through the normal hit resolver, so avoidance and dash shielding still matter; this is not guaranteed damage or an unavoidable server effect. The warning begins before detonation, not at spawn. <small>[shell-4DJTZFjO.js: `` fuseSec:6,blastLeadSec:.8,blastRadius:180 ``]</small> <small>[titan.hot-YpLC4NDB.js: `` r*r+a*a<=T.blastRadius*T.blastRadius ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-DwGb2BE_.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-endgame-roles.mjs` used original phase constructors, spawners and consumers with supplied roles, clocks, positions and HP. These are client-rule checks, not a naturally reached encounter, survival advice, Nightmare timing or server verification. The fixture checked original totem spawning, zero speed, no early blast, detonation at the six-second boundary and destroyed-totem cancellation. Blast damage, warning rendering and natural-run survival were not measured.

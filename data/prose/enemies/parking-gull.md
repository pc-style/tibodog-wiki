## Overview

The Parking Lot Gull is a tier-1 pest of the [Fry Palace Drive-Thru](/biomes/fry-palace) landmark, alongside the [Crumb Ant](/enemies/crumb-ant) (“Gulls and raccoons rule the parking lot.”). Tier 1 makes up most spawns in the opening minutes and leaves the wave table after minute 6; with Normal's two-minute [tier lag](/modes) that means up to about 8:59.

It is the fastest tier-1 pest at speed 48 and one of the frailest at 7 HP, with 3 contact damage. It gives 1 XP and has a 4% chance to drop gold; Fry Palace coins are worth ×1.2.

It is a different enemy from the [Gull Flock](/enemies/gull-flock) that crosses the lot during the Fry Palace's [Lunch Rush](/events/lunch-rush) event.

## How it works

It has no special behaviour: the `chase` kind just uses the default movement straight towards Tibo. <small>[sim-BEjq2fOA.js: `` case`chase`:case`tank`:case`splitter`:return; ``]</small> With mass 0 it takes full knockback.

## Tips

- **Kill it instead of retreating.** Its speed means it catches up with a retreating Tibo sooner than other tier-1 pests, but at 7 HP it is the easiest of them to kill.

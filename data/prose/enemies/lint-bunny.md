## Overview

The Lint Bunny is the tier-1 pest of the [Coin Laundromat](/biomes/coin-laundromat) landmark and its only one. Three more come out of every [Sock Pile](/enemies/sock-pile) that dies. Tier 1 makes up most spawns in the opening minutes and leaves the wave table after minute 6; with Normal's two-minute [tier lag](/modes) that means up to about 8:59. Bunnies from Sock Piles can appear as long as piles do.

It is basic fodder: it walks straight at Tibo at speed 36 and has 8 HP and 3 contact damage. It gives 1 XP. Its 5% gold chance is the highest of any tier-1 pest (most have 3–4%), and the Laundromat's coins are worth ×1.4.

## How it works

It has no special behaviour: the `chase` kind just uses the default movement straight towards Tibo. <small>[sim-BsDYg9eQ.js: `` case`chase`:case`tank`:case`splitter`:return; ``]</small> With mass 0 it takes full knockback.

Lint Bunnies released by a Sock Pile take over the pile's HP, damage and speed scaling, so late-run bunnies are tougher than the base 8 HP suggests.

## Tips

- **Sweep them up in the Laundromat.** It is frail, so its threat is numbers rather than any single bunny. In the Laundromat it can pay to sweep them up for the extra gold.

## Overview

The Sock Pile is the only tier-2 pest of the [Coin Laundromat](/biomes/coin-laundromat) landmark. Tier 2 is in the wave table from the start until minute 9, which with Normal's two-minute [tier lag](/modes) means up to about 11:59.

It walks straight at Tibo at speed 30, with 24 HP and 6 contact damage. When killed it bursts into three [Lint Bunnies](/enemies/lint-bunny). The pile gives 2 XP and each bunny gives its normal 1 XP, so a Sock Pile is worth 5 XP in total. Each of the four can also drop gold: 6% for the pile, 5% for each bunny, and the Laundromat's coins are worth ×1.4.

## How it works

On death the pile spawns three Lint Bunnies in a ring around the spot, pushed outward. Unlike a [Snow Mold](/enemies/snow-mold), which splits into weaker copies of itself, a splitter with a named child spawns full Lint Bunnies. The bunnies take over the pile's HP, damage and speed scaling, applied to the Lint Bunny's own base stats (8 HP, 3 damage). <small>[sim-HQr6OS1G.js: `ja(e,o?r:a,c+6*g(d),l+6*u(d))`]</small>

An elite Sock Pile (tier 2 is the elite tier from 1:00 to 4:59) releases ordinary Lint Bunnies, not elite ones. No bunnies are made once the enemy limit (550) is reached.

## Tips

- Each pile turns into three more pests, so killing piles at a distance gives the bunnies time to be caught by area weapons before they reach Tibo.

## How it works

This is the Court’s **brood helper**. Spawns up to **two Court crowd pests every 5.6 seconds**, only while the phase can spawn and there is room below the 40-member Court cap. That count includes registered helpers and attendants, not just crowd pests. It checks the cap before spawning and limits its children to the remaining room. This is not an on-death split. <small>[court-DBkbiawc.js: `` broodEverySec:e(7,1200),broodCount:2 ``]</small>

It still walks toward Tibo through the ordinary chase/maze movement path. The helper role replaces catalog stats with speed **30**, base HP **18000 × the spawn HP scale** and XP 60. Its advantage is the support rule above, not a separate ranged attack. Aura checks use centre-to-centre distance, include equality, and only apply to registered Court members. <small>[endgame-sim-Bth1VwWq.js: `` c=A.helperHp,l=A.helperSpeed,u=A.helperXp ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-Bth1VwWq.js: `` return a*a+o*o<=r*r ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-DwGb2BE_.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-endgame-roles.mjs` used original phase constructors, spawners and consumers with supplied roles, clocks, positions and HP. These are client-rule checks, not a naturally reached encounter, survival advice, Nightmare timing or server verification. The fixture checked original helper spawning, speed override, both sides of its brood timer, exactly two children without repeated same-tick spawning, one remaining place and saturation at 40 registered members.

## Overview

The Ketchup Drop is a tier-1 pest of [Ketchup Bog](/biomes/ketchup-bog) and [Mash Swamp](/biomes/mash-swamp), sharing the tier with the [Bog Slime](/enemies/bog-slime) in both. Tier 1 makes up most spawns in the opening minutes and leaves the wave table after minute 6; with Normal's two-minute [tier lag](/modes) that means up to about 8:59.

It is slow fodder: it walks straight at Tibo at speed 30 and has 9 HP and 4 contact damage. It gives 1 XP and has a 3% chance to drop gold; Ketchup Bog coins are worth ×1.3. Its stats are identical to the [Oil Drip](/enemies/oil-drip) of the fry biomes.

## How it works

It has no special behaviour: the `chase` kind just uses the default movement straight towards Tibo. <small>[sim-D_PHN0Cx.js: `` case`chase`:case`tank`:case`splitter`:return; ``]</small> With mass 0 it takes full knockback.

## Tips

- At speed 30 it is less than half Tibo's base speed of 62, so it only becomes a problem in numbers. In Ketchup Bog, keep an eye on the burning ketchup puddles (“Puddles of ketchup burn and slow.”) as well.

## Overview

The Cave Grub is the tier-2 pest of [Compost Caves](/biomes/compost-caves), and the only one, so every tier-2 spawn in the caves is a grub. Tier-2 pests trickle in from the first minute, peak around 5:00 and stop spawning at 12:00 on Normal (13:00 on Easy). Between 1:00 and 4:59 the minute's [elite](/bosses#elites) is a Cave Grub if Tibo is in the caves.

It crawls straight at Tibo at speed 32 and hurts only on contact, for 6 base damage. With 24 base HP it is the toughest tier-2 pest that simply chases, ahead of the [Potato Beetle](/enemies/potato-beetle) and [Tomato Leech](/enemies/tomato-leech) (20 each). At tier 2 only the [Garden Slug](/enemies/garden-slug) (34) and the charging [Bowling Ball](/enemies/bowling-ball) (28) have more. It is also the slowest of those chasers, and its mass of 0.2 trims knockback by 20%. A kill gives 2 XP and a 5% chance of a coin.

## Tips

- **Grubs need about three times the damage.** Grubs take about three times as much damage to kill as the [Compost Maggots](/enemies/compost-maggot) that make up the caves' tier-1 spawns. When the mix shifts towards grubs around 5:00, weapons that were one-shotting maggots may stop keeping up.
- At speed 32 they are about half as fast as Tibo, so walking away while your weapons work is usually safe.

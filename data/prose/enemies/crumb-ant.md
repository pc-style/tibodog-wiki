## Overview

The Crumb Ant is the tier-1 pest of the fried-food biomes. It shares the tier-1 slot with the [Oil Drip](/enemies/oil-drip) in [Chip Canyons](/biomes/chip-canyons) and [Fry-Oil Desert](/biomes/fry-oil-desert), with the [Parking Lot Gull](/enemies/parking-gull) in the [Fry Palace Drive-Thru](/biomes/fry-palace), and with the [Bowling Pin](/enemies/bowling-pin) in [Gutterball Lanes](/biomes/gutterball-lanes). On Normal, tier-1 pests make up 8 in 9 spawns until 4:00 and stop spawning at 9:00 (10:00 on Easy). As a tier-1 pest it is never an [elite](/bosses#elites).

It runs straight at Tibo and hurts only on contact, for 3 base damage. It is built for speed, not toughness: 6 base HP, tied with the [Aphid](/enemies/aphid) for the lowest of any pest, and speed 46, second only to the Parking Lot Gull among tier-1 pests. A kill gives 1 XP and a 3% chance of a coin.

## Tips

- **Ants arrive first in early waves.** Ants reach Tibo well before their slower biome partners (Oil Drips and Bowling Pins move at 30). Expect them to arrive first in any early wave.
- With so little HP (about 4 at the start of a Normal run), weapons that fire often or pierce tend to clear ants better than slow, heavy hitters.

## How it works

This is the Court’s **banner helper**. Moves as a helper and raises **other nearby Court members’ movement velocity to ×1.5** within 82 units. It does not boost itself; overlapping banners do not multiply the same member again in that tick. Killing the banner removes this moving aura. <small>[court-BqtnWTEX.js: `` bannerRadius:82,bannerSpeedMult:1.5 ``]</small>

It still walks toward Tibo through the ordinary chase/maze movement path. The helper role replaces catalog stats with speed **30**, base HP **18000 × the spawn HP scale** and XP 60. Its advantage is the support rule above, not a separate ranged attack. Aura checks use centre-to-centre distance, include equality, and only apply to registered Court members. <small>[endgame-sim-Cz95Rt58.js: `` c=A.helperHp,l=A.helperSpeed,u=A.helperXp ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-Cz95Rt58.js: `` return a*a+o*o<=r*r ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-DwGb2BE_.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-endgame-roles.mjs` used original phase constructors, spawners and consumers with supplied roles, clocks, positions and HP. These are client-rule checks, not a naturally reached encounter, survival advice, Nightmare timing or server verification. The fixture checked original helper spawning, speed override and nearby versus outside-range support. Mender pickup collection and overlapping-helper combinations are source-audited, not measured here.

## How it works

This is the **rat pack's alpha**, despite the catalog placing it among ordinary enemies. It can spawn as an ordinary leader or as an elite. It circles and strafes around a standoff distance of 105 rather than blindly walking into Tibo. Both roles can lunge; **only the elite fires the projectile pattern below**. <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` r.nextLunge[c]=e.time+3.5 ``]</small>

When the relevant pack/individual timers permit, a target **at or inside 75 units** starts a stationary 0.8-second Normal crouch. Aim is fixed then, with a lead from Tibo's velocity (sampled and capped at 70 units/s), not continuously homed during flight. The leap's distance is the predicted target distance plus 20, clamped between 90 and 110; its speed is **200 units/s**, so 110 is a maximum travel distance, not the trigger range. <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` c.leapLen[n]=he(f+20,90,a.distance),c.leapSpeed[n]=a.speed ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` c>70&&(a*=70/c,s*=70/c) ``]</small>

During the leap it is **invincible** and contact damage switches to its scaled lunge damage (raw 16, versus ordinary raw contact 2). At the end it loses invincibility, restores ordinary contact damage and stops for 0.8 s of recovery. An alpha gets a further 3.5-second individual lunge delay after recovery. A recognised solid prop in the next movement step interrupts a leap and uses a separate **0.8-second recovery**. This does not mean every decorative object or every obstruction blocks it. <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` r.damage[o]=a.leapDmg[o],r.set(o,i.Invincible) ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` ir(e,n,r)}(e,t,i,g.rockStunSec) ``]</small>

Killing an alpha scatters its surviving pack: members initially flee for 1.5–2 s at 55% speed, then may circle at 25% speed before resuming chase/lunge. The return is staggered by member order (0.45 s per earlier member), not a permanent fear effect. A slot-generation check prevents a replacement enemy from inheriting a dead pack member's role. <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` n.chaseAt[r]=n.fleeUntil[r]+.45*n.order[a] ``]</small> <small>[titan.hot-DjIkZXtH.js: `` e.memberId[n]!==t.enemies.pool.id(n)?v.none:e.role[n] ``]</small>

The catalog HP/speed is not the whole Hunt encounter. The actual Hunt release spawner applies a speed floor of 1.1 × Tibo's configured walking speed × movement multiplier and replaces member HP with a difficulty/time/ladder-scaled Hunt budget. Alpha HP also depends on leader versus elite status and curse. These spawn overrides do not change the lunge's listed speed. <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` r.speed[o]=Math.max(r.speed[o]??0,i) ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` ot(n,e,o)===x.member&&(r.maxHp[o]=Cr(e),r.hp[o]=r.maxHp[o]??0) ``]</small>

### Elite projectile attack

Rat Chief alternates three aimed single shots (speed 120, 0.3 s apart) with a three-shot fan (speed 110, total arc 0.7 radians). The rest after the final scheduled burst is 1.4 s. It only starts a volley at distances 60–170. Every pattern has a **0.8-second telegraph**. The first engagement within 170 units delays firing by at least 0.4 s. Shots use the ordinary emitter/bullet path; they are not instant damage. The ordinary path admits a volley only when **currently visible bullets + proposed count × bursts ≤ 45**. This does not reserve all other pending emitters or guarantee a global maximum of 45 bullets. The separate Burrow budget applies when active; a refused volley retries after 0.5 s. After a successful cast the alpha reduces its steering-speed factor to 30% through the scheduled firing period plus 0.2 s; the added player-velocity lead is separate. Normal leaders have none of these projectile volleys. <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` if(n.alphaKind[i]===xt.elite&&p&&ve(e,u,d)&&Br(e,t,n,r) ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` l(e)+t.count*n<=45 ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` n.patternNext[i]=t.time+.5 ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-BMdkCjMQ.js: `` telegraph:Or ``]</small>

## How it works

This is **attendant 2 of the Queen**, not a standalone scheduled boss. The three attendants use the same protection role; their names do not confer banner, mender or shell-helper auras. Each spawns 48 units from the Queen at its own third of the circle, then uses the ordinary chase/maze movement path. Court spawning gives speed **34**, base HP **12000 × the spawn HP scale** and XP 120, so the catalog HP of 1 is not its encounter HP. <small>[endgame-sim-CYNUrOik.js: `` let i=n/3*v ``]</small> <small>[court-tEbZPZnO.js: `` attendantOrbitRadius:48 ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-CYNUrOik.js: `` c=A.attendantHp,l=A.attendantSpeed,u=A.attendantXp ``]</small>

**Any surviving attendant protects the Queen.** Killing only this one is not enough if either other attendant remains. Once all three are gone, the Queen is vulnerable. A defeated attendant schedules a return after 18.75 s; a warning is placed near the Queen before it reappears. This is a window to attack the Queen, not permanent removal. <small>[endgame-sim-CYNUrOik.js: `` for(let t=0;t<3;t++)if(la(e,n,t))return n.stats.queenBlocks++,!0;return!1 ``]</small> <small>[court-tEbZPZnO.js: `` attendantReturnSec:e(1,48) ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-DwGb2BE_.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-endgame-roles.mjs` used original phase constructors, spawners and consumers with supplied roles, clocks, positions and HP. These are client-rule checks, not a naturally reached encounter, survival advice, Nightmare timing or server verification. The fixture checked attendant spawning, speed override, real Queen protection with attendants alive versus all gone, and 18.75-second return scheduling. Warning placement and return animation were not exercised.

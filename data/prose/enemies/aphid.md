## Overview

The Aphid is a tier-1 pest of [Potato Field](/biomes/potato-field), [Peel Forest](/biomes/peel-forest) and [Crop Circle](/biomes/crop-circle). Each of those biomes has two tier-1 pests, and the game picks between them at random, so about half of the early spawns there are Aphids (the other half are [Sprout Mites](/enemies/sprout-mite), or [Little Greys](/enemies/little-grey) in Crop Circle). Every run starts in Potato Field, so Aphids are usually among the first pests Tibo meets. On Normal, tier-1 pests make up 8 in 9 spawns until 4:00, become a minority from 5:00 and stop spawning at 9:00 (one minute later on Easy, which has a longer [tier lag](/bosses#elites)).

It has no attack of its own: it walks straight at Tibo and hurts only by touching. With 6 base HP it is tied with the [Crumb Ant](/enemies/crumb-ant) as the most fragile pest in the game, and at speed 42 it is one of the quicker tier-1 pests (only the Crumb Ant and the [Parking Lot Gull](/enemies/parking-gull) are faster). A kill gives 1 XP and a 3% chance of a coin.

[Elites](/bosses#elites) are drawn from tier 2 and up, with one exception that can make an elite Aphid. Crop Circle's only tier-2 pest is the shooting [Spittlebug](/enemies/spittlebug), and when the shooter cap is full (2 shooters before 3:00, 4 before 6:00) the pick falls back to the tier below. <small>[sim-DS2NHJj-.js: `if(Ms[i]===0||Fs(e)<Ps(e))return i`]</small>

## Tips

- Aphids arrive while enemy damage is at its lowest: on Normal the damage multiplier is about ×0.22 at the start and ×0.35 at 5:00 (see [Enemy scaling](/enemy-scaling)), so a touch costs Tibo about 1 HP or less before armor. You can afford to walk through a thin line of them to reach a pickup.
- They have no mass, so any weapon with knockback pushes them the full distance.

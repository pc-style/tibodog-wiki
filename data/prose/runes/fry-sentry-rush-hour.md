## How it works

Rush Hour doubles turrets per drop and doubles the drop cooldown, while scaling damage ×0.78. There is a second consumer interaction: salvo sets computed **Interval to zero**, and turrets read that same value as their firing interval. Their minimum clamp therefore makes each turret fire every **6 ticks (0.1 s)** in this ordinary fixture, not the normal 21 ticks. Supplied Amount bonus 0/3 creates **2/8 turrets at once**, then waits 12 s for another drop. This is neither two delayed drops nor an unchanged per-turret firing rate. <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` id:`fry-sentry-rush-hour` ``]</small>

The summon system reads the weapon’s **current stats when firing**, not a damage snapshot from when it spawned. Changing supplied damage multiplier to 1.5 affects the same retained summon’s next bolt. Each summon emits **one bolt** when its timer is ready; Amount controls summon count, not bolts per summon shot. No eligible target means a **6-tick retry**, verified by removing all targets and returning them during the wait. Six shots per group check real aim, shot spacing, payload and original-resolver HP loss; projectile contacts are forced at the chosen pest, not natural flight. <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` s!==i&&(wP(e,s,VP),i=s) ``]</small> <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` if(p===void 0){n.t[o]=HP;continue} ``]</small>

Each drop places all computed Amount turrets **18 units from Tibo** in random directions; there are no within-drop timed waves. A turret stays still and expires before shooting once its lifetime runs out. Bolts have **1.2 s lifetime** regardless of the turret’s own Duration. The firing timer uses **max(0.1 × the ordinary mode floor, computed Interval)**, not the drop cooldown; the fixture uses the ordinary floor of 1. Lifetime controls isolate one retained turret rather than claiming natural overlap totals. <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` for(let i=0;i<n.amount;i++) ``]</small> <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` r.x[a]=e.player.x[e.me]+18*k(o) ``]</small> <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` let m=l?Math.max(.1*Bx(e),VP.interval):VP.cooldown ``]</small> <small>[sim-CSAZ0MJi.js: `` e.projectiles.life[h]=1.2,e.projectiles.maxLife[h]=1.2 ``]</small>

## How it works

Hopper scales direct-hit damage ×0.66 and adds a **fuse blast plus pierce-limited hopping**. With no pierce bonus, the first contact queues direct damage and immediately detonates: the struck pest loses **7.92 + 11.88 = 19.8 HP** in the supplied level-one fixture, while a nearby pest receives only the **11.88** blast. With Pierce bonus 2, the first two contacts consume pierce and redirect toward the nearest unhit eligible pest within the 80-unit hop search; the third contact detonates. It is not a blast on every hop. With spare pierce but no hop target, the projectile survives with a reduced pierce count rather than detonating immediately. Expiration separately triggers the blast without a direct contact. <small>[sim-CTc3ZIOi.js: `` id:`seeker-sprouts-hopper` ``]</small>

Launches start around the facing direction with a random **±1.2-radian** offset, before Burst’s ring override. Ordinary steering refreshes on **(tick + projectile slot) mod 6 = 0**, searches within **200 units** of the projectile, and turns at most **5/60 radians per tick** at level one while preserving speed. The retained controls deliberately reset one projectile’s position/velocity and advance the original movement consumer; they do not simulate a complete flight to a moving pest. <small>[sim-CTc3ZIOi.js: `` OM(e)+e.rng.range(-1.2,1.2) ``]</small> <small>[sim-CTc3ZIOi.js: `` if((e.tick+t)%6!=0)return ``]</small> <small>[sim-CTc3ZIOi.js: `` let i=e.loadout.bySlot(t);return i!==void 0&&Jk(i)?qk(e,n,r,200,0):ud(e,n,r,200) ``]</small>

Hopper’s blast radius is **26 × Area**, with enemy-body overlap, and blast damage is **1.5 × projectile damage**. Its hop search remains 80 units; Area does not scale that distance. The original contact resolver is exercised with Pierce bonus **0 and 2**, manually placing the projectile on successive separated pests so direct damage and blast damage cannot be confused. A separate expiry-before-movement control verifies blast-only HP loss. These forced contacts establish fuse rules, not autonomous hop travel or encounter totals. <small>[sim-CTc3ZIOi.js: `` i.fuseRadius[t]=n.radius*r,i.fuseRange[t]=n.hopRange ``]</small> <small>[sim-CTc3ZIOi.js: `` let r=e.projectiles,i=r.pierce[t],a=i>0?hA(e,t,n,r.fuseRange[t]):-1 ``]</small> <small>[sim-CTc3ZIOi.js: `` r.pierce[t]=i-1,pA(r,t) ``]</small> <small>[sim-CTc3ZIOi.js: `` Y(e,r.damage[t]*r.fuseBlast[t],r.src[t],r.slot[t],r.knockback[t]) ``]</small>

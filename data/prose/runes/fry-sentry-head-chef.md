## How it works

Head Chef concentrates computed Amount into **one larger, stronger turret**. Before focus it scales damage ×1.6 and Interval ×0.62. If N is pre-focus Amount, damage gains **1 + 0.9 × (N − 1)** and Area gains **1 + 0.7 × (N − 1)**, then Amount becomes 1. These bonuses add per spare turret, not compound. The isolated turret fires every **13 ticks (about 0.2167 s)** in the supplied fixture, rather than the ordinary 21 ticks. This does not change the drop cooldown or turret lifetime. <small>[sim-MzgJYBwV.js: `` id:`fry-sentry-head-chef` ``]</small>

The summon system reads the weapon’s **current stats when firing**, not a damage snapshot from when it spawned. Changing supplied damage multiplier to 1.5 affects the same retained summon’s next bolt. Each summon emits **one bolt** when its timer is ready; Amount controls summon count, not bolts per summon shot. No eligible target means a **6-tick retry**, verified by removing all targets and returning them during the wait. Six shots per group check real aim, shot spacing, payload and original-resolver HP loss; projectile contacts are forced at the chosen pest, not natural flight. <small>[sim-MzgJYBwV.js: `` s!==i&&(CM(e,s,BM),i=s) ``]</small> <small>[sim-MzgJYBwV.js: `` if(p===void 0){n.t[o]=VM;continue} ``]</small>

Each drop places all computed Amount turrets **18 units from Tibo** in random directions; there are no within-drop timed waves. A turret stays still and expires before shooting once its lifetime runs out. Bolts have **1.2 s lifetime** regardless of the turret’s own Duration. The firing timer uses **max(0.1 × the ordinary mode floor, computed Interval)**, not the drop cooldown; the fixture uses the ordinary floor of 1. Lifetime controls isolate one retained turret rather than claiming natural overlap totals. <small>[sim-MzgJYBwV.js: `` for(let i=0;i<n.amount;i++) ``]</small> <small>[sim-MzgJYBwV.js: `` r.x[a]=e.player.x[e.me]+18*O(o) ``]</small> <small>[sim-MzgJYBwV.js: `` let m=l?Math.max(.1*sb(e),BM.interval):BM.cooldown ``]</small> <small>[sim-MzgJYBwV.js: `` e.projectiles.life[g]=1.2,e.projectiles.maxLife[g]=1.2 ``]</small>

## How it works

These are **forms of one Burrow boss**, not four separately scheduled bosses or four fresh HP bars. The chain is Root Hydra → Mire → Stump → Frenzy. It advances at 70%, 45% and 30% of its existing maximum HP, including equality, or when the elapsed-time stage reaches 10.5, 21 and 31.5 s since its arrival. Either condition can advance it; the controller advances at most one form per tick. Changing form keeps the same body, current/max HP, stored speed and radius. The later catalog HP of 900000 is not loaded as a heal. <small>[endgame-sim-odN1Dfj1.js: `` r.def[i]=o,r.state[i]=0,r.timer[i]=T.hydraFormPauseSec ``]</small> <small>[shell-DrgOb1Wl.js: `` hydraBelow:[.7,.45,.3] ``]</small> <small>[shell-DrgOb1Wl.js: `` hydraFormSec:d(7,600),hydraFormPauseSec:1.5 ``]</small>

The charge captures its direction toward Tibo when the **0.9-second warning starts**. Moving afterward does not turn the charge. It then sets velocity to **330 units/s for 0.9 s**, a nominal 297-unit travel before obstructions. That is not an extra trigger radius or guaranteed damage. <small>[sim-CKlUbSnG.js: `` o.tx[t]=r/s,o.ty[t]=i/s ``]</small> <small>[sim-CKlUbSnG.js: `` i.vx[t]=i.tx[t]*a.dashSpeed,i.vy[t]=i.ty[t]*a.dashSpeed ``]</small> Its main cooldown is 2.6 s. The independent pattern list cycles three three-shot aimed bursts, a 16-shot ring and two seven-shot fans, at a base 1.8-second pattern interval. This form has no further HP-triggered transformation; its displayed 900000 HP does not create a second health bar. Projectile travel and real hits are not measured here.

A form change resets the main and bullet timers to **1.5 s** and clears the attack state/windup. Contact damage is recalculated from the original Hydra's damage, current run scaling and the Burrow ×0.6 factor, then the form multipliers **1, 1, 3, 4.25**. It does not simply use each form's raw contact-damage row. Run-time scaling can still change this base while the boss remains alive. <small>[endgame-sim-odN1Dfj1.js: `` r.damage[i]=Qr(e,a) ``]</small> <small>[shell-DrgOb1Wl.js: `` hydraFormDamage:[1,1,3,4.25],bulletDamage:.6 ``]</small>

If the main Hydra stays off-screen long enough and is idle, its family can relocate it near Tibo, hide it briefly and delay attacks for its reappearance. That is not a permanent disappearance or a fresh boss. Resurfacing, Burrow bullet-budget contention and natural phase rhythm are source-audited, not measured by these form controls. <small>[endgame-sim-odN1Dfj1.js: `` t.stats.hydraResurfaced++ ``]</small>

Starting weapon removed; no give/god/warp or reward-service writes. These are client-rule fixtures, not naturally reached encounters, integrated movement, actual damage hits, Nightmare, survival/DPS or server/device verification. The fixture separately drives HP and elapsed-time thresholds, checking just before and exactly at each boundary, persistent HP/body stats, form attack construction and reset timers. Same-clock HP controls check the 1/1/3/4.25 damage factors. Pool/minion completion, complete movement and projectile hits are not measured.

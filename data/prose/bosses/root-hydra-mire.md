## How it works

These are **forms of one Burrow boss**, not four separately scheduled bosses or four fresh HP bars. The chain is Root Hydra → Mire → Stump → Frenzy. It advances at 70%, 45% and 30% of its existing maximum HP, including equality, or when the elapsed-time stage reaches 10.5, 21 and 31.5 s since its arrival. Either condition can advance it; the controller advances at most one form per tick. Changing form keeps the same body, current/max HP, stored speed and radius. The later catalog HP of 900000 is not loaded as a heal. <small>[endgame-sim-iaCOEzdG.js: `` r.def[i]=o,r.state[i]=0,r.timer[i]=w.hydraFormPauseSec ``]</small> <small>[shell-Czi9QN9-.js: `` hydraBelow:[.7,.45,.3] ``]</small> <small>[shell-Czi9QN9-.js: `` hydraFormSec:d(7,600),hydraFormPauseSec:1.5 ``]</small>

Mire places **six delayed splat pools**, one centred on Tibo's captured position and the others scattered within its 200-unit spread. Their radius is 34, warning 1.2 s and pool lifetime 7 s. The warning hit uses half the current boss contact damage; the lingering rate starts from raw 14 DPS scaled by current boss damage / this form's raw damage. Thus raw 14 is not guaranteed final damage after mitigation. Its separate projectile pattern is a seven-shot fan in two bursts. Pool hit/tick resolution and projectile travel are source-audited, not measured here. <small>[sim-Cb_K_V3L.js: `` e.zones.afterLife[f]=i.poolTime,e.zones.afterDps[f]=i.poolDps*(s/n.damage) ``]</small>

A form change resets the main and bullet timers to **1.5 s** and clears the attack state/windup. Contact damage is recalculated from the original Hydra's damage, current run scaling and the Burrow ×0.6 factor, then the form multipliers **1, 1, 3, 4.25**. It does not simply use each form's raw contact-damage row. Run-time scaling can still change this base while the boss remains alive. <small>[endgame-sim-iaCOEzdG.js: `` r.damage[i]=Zr(e,a) ``]</small> <small>[shell-Czi9QN9-.js: `` hydraFormDamage:[1,1,3,4.25],bulletDamage:.6 ``]</small>

If the main Hydra stays off-screen long enough and is idle, its family can relocate it near Tibo, hide it briefly and delay attacks for its reappearance. That is not a permanent disappearance or a fresh boss. Resurfacing, Burrow bullet-budget contention and natural phase rhythm are source-audited, not measured by these form controls. <small>[endgame-sim-iaCOEzdG.js: `` t.stats.hydraResurfaced++ ``]</small>

These are client-rule fixtures, not naturally reached encounters, integrated movement, actual damage hits, Nightmare, survival/DPS or server/device verification. The fixture separately drives HP and elapsed-time thresholds, checking just before and exactly at each boundary, persistent HP/body stats, form attack construction and reset timers. Same-clock HP controls check the 1/1/3/4.25 damage factors. Pool/minion completion, complete movement and projectile hits are not measured.

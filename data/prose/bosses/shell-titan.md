## How it works

While registered as the active Shell boss, the Titan receives **10% of ordinary incoming damage**. Completing a charge opens **3 seconds of full damage**, after which protection returns. Exactly at the closing deadline it is protected again. The plate index advances once on closure and wraps through three plates; the damage gate does **not** inspect the direction of the incoming hit. The plates are not three directional weak spots you must aim at. <small>[titan.hot-pi1L2FMI.js: `` return $(e,t)===Q.protected?T.protectedDamage:1 ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-CYG3250_.js: `` t.titanPlate=(t.titanPlate+1)%en.plateCount ``]</small> <small>[shell-CJW0vWqS.js: `` plateOpeningSec:3,plateCount:3 ``]</small>

The charge captures its direction toward Tibo when the **1-second warning starts**. Moving afterward does not turn the charge. It then sets velocity to **210 units/s for 0.8 s**, a nominal 168-unit travel before obstructions. That is not an extra trigger radius or guaranteed damage. <small>[sim-CCvdOWKf.js: `` o.tx[t]=r/s,o.ty[t]=i/s ``]</small> <small>[sim-CCvdOWKf.js: `` i.vx[t]=i.tx[t]*a.dashSpeed,i.vy[t]=i.ty[t]*a.dashSpeed ``]</small>

The opening comes from the charge's active-to-idle transition, not from firing a bullet pattern. Its independent pattern list cycles a seven-shot fan, five aimed shots and a ten-shot ring; the catalog's 4.6 s is the base pattern interval. Aimed shots are projectiles, not unavoidable direct damage. Shell beasts, their individual armour openings and timed Fuse Totems remain separate threats; opening the Titan does not disable them. <small>[endgame-sim-CYG3250_.js: `` t.titanAttackState===2&&n===0 ``]</small>

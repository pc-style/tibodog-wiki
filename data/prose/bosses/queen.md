## How it works

The Court's Queen is protected by **any surviving one of her three attendants**. All three must be gone to remove that protection; her raw HP row does not describe this gate. Attendants spawn 48 units from her in thirds of a circle. Their names do not grant the separate banner, mender or shell-helper auras. <small>[endgame-sim-u-MHepb2.js: `` for(let t=0;t<3;t++)if(ua(e,n,t))return n.stats.queenBlocks++,!0;return!1 ``]</small> <small>[court-OP7kamK1.js: `` attendantOrbitRadius:48 ``]</small>

Each lost attendant schedules its return **18.75 s after the controller notices its absence**. A warning is placed near the Queen 0.8 s before the deadline; only the returned attendant, not the warning itself, restores protection. If warning creation is delayed or its zone cannot be created, the controller can postpone the return; 18.75 s is not a guaranteed maximum vulnerability window. <small>[court-OP7kamK1.js: `` attendantReturnSec:e(1,48),attendantReturnWarnSec:.8 ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-u-MHepb2.js: `` t.attendantReturnAt[n]=e.runClock+oa(e).attendantReturnWarnSec ``]</small>

Her ground sweep captures Tibo's position and direction at placement. It creates **three parallel lanes**, each full width 42 and length 320, with lane centres 109.2 units apart. Their warnings end at 0.8, 0.95 and 1.1 s, not simultaneously. After her sweep warning completes, the main attack timer resets to 4 s. Her separate ring pattern schedules two 15-bullet bursts, 0.5 s apart at speed 76, after a 0.8-second warning; it is not a second set of ground lanes. <small>[sim-CQ8ALNnZ.js: `` let f=(d-(i.lanes-1)/2)*i.width*2.6 ``]</small> <small>[sim-CQ8ALNnZ.js: `` n.telegraph+.15*d ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-8zbhOvtE.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-endgame-bosses.mjs` uses original family spawners, attack and family consumers with supplied clocks, timers, positions and HP. Starting weapon removed; no give/god/warp or reward-service writes. These are client-rule fixtures, not naturally reached encounters, integrated movement, actual damage hits, Nightmare, survival/DPS or server/device verification. The fixture checks three lane constructions and their warning offsets, the last-attendant versus no-attendants gate, invincibility removal, return/warning boundaries and protection on return. Bullet travel, lane hit resolution and maze navigation remain source-audited rather than measured.

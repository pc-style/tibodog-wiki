## How it works

This is a **temporary stationary Burrow head**, not an extra scheduled Hydra or the final body. Its controller starts with a 1-second splash warning, creates a hidden body, then reveals it after a further 0.6 s. HP comes from the elite/time/curse/run-scaling path, not the catalog's raw 2000 alone. Its position is held by the head controller. <small>[endgame-sim-CtgyYs8d.js: `` splashSec:1,riseSec:.6,sinkSec:.7 ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-CtgyYs8d.js: `` r.speed[a]=0,r.damage[a]=o.damage*e.cfg.level.damage ``]</small>

The head uses its own short attack plan: level-gated **Mud Bloom rings, Dirt Wall fans, Spit Lanes or Root Whirl spirals**, and, when selected, a ground sweep. The ordinary catalog pattern timer is parked, so its raw single-ring row is not a promise that every head fires only that ring every 3 s. The plan coordinates firing/rest windows with other heads, the visible/pending projectile budget and its sink deadline. The first planned move's warning is at least 1 s. A head that times out sinks and can release a pack; driving it away follows a separate reward path. Natural plan selection, other moves, full sinking/pack release and rewards are source-audited, not exercised by this fixture. <small>[endgame-sim-CtgyYs8d.js: `` r.timer[a]=Fi,r.patternT[a]=Fi ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-CtgyYs8d.js: `` t&&e.at===0?Math.max(e.pattern.telegraph,W.firstLeadSec):e.pattern.telegraph ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-CtgyYs8d.js: `` id:`root-whirl`,family:`whirl` ``]</small>

Damage recorded against heads **chips the future main Hydra's HP budget**, rather than spawning a new full-strength boss after each head. Newly observed lost HP is counted once, divided by the planned main-boss HP basis and capped at a cumulative 25% before the main Hydra arrives. Main-body spawning uses the remaining share and removes temporary heads. Damage after the main body is up cannot increase that pre-arrival chip budget. <small>[endgame-sim-CtgyYs8d.js: `` t.chipped=Math.min(t.chipCap,t.chipped+a/t.chipOf) ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-CtgyYs8d.js: `` chipCap:t.hydraUp?Vi(e):T.chipCap ``]</small> <small>[endgame-sim-CtgyYs8d.js: `` hpMult:(1-i)*T.hydraHpMult ``]</small>

Controlled checks on `main-8zbhOvtE.js`, Standard/Normal, seeds `s1/s2/s3`: `node tools/probe-endgame-bosses.mjs` uses original family spawners, attack and family consumers with supplied clocks, timers, positions and HP. Starting weapon removed; no give/god/warp or reward-service writes. These are client-rule fixtures, not naturally reached encounters, integrated movement, actual damage hits, Nightmare, survival/DPS or server/device verification. The head fixture selects first-tier Mud Bloom explicitly and checks its original 14-shot, speed-62 emitter with a 1-second warning, plus splash/rise deadlines, hidden-to-visible transition and stationary speed. It supplies 1000 head HP and a 1000-HP bookkeeping basis to isolate lost-HP shares without entering the defeat/reward branch, checks duplicate/cap accounting, removes heads on main-body spawn and compares the body's 75%-HP result with an unchipped control. It does not measure actual weapon hits, natural chip acquisition, plan selection or reward delivery.
